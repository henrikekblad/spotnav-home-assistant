SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.5.7 fixes a planned charge that stayed stopped after load balancing stopped it to protect the main fuse.

## Fixes

- **A planned charge stopped to protect the main fuse starts again when there is room.** When the house suddenly takes more current (a home battery that starts charging from the grid, an oven) and the charger cannot lower its current in time, load balancing stops the charge to protect the main fuse. This is a safety stop. For a charge you started yourself, SpotNav already started it again once there was room. A charge started by the plan, or a top-off, stayed stopped until the next charging window, even when the load was gone within minutes. This happened most on chargers that accept a new current only every few seconds, such as an Alfen over Modbus. Now SpotNav starts these charges again the same way. It waits at least five minutes after the safety stop, and every phase the charger uses must have room for its minimum current plus one ampere for a minute. If the charge has to be stopped for safety again during the same plug-in, each new stop doubles the wait (10 minutes, then 20, at most 30), so a load that keeps coming back cannot switch the charger on and off all night. Unplugging the car starts again from five minutes. A charging window that ends while the charge is stopped still ends it.
- **A charge stopped by load balancing is no longer mistaken for the car ending it.** If the charger reported the charge as stopped before load balancing had finished its stop, SpotNav could read it as the car ending the charge by itself. That ended your Start, or recorded the car as done for the window, often with the car far from full. SpotNav now knows the stop is load balancing's from the moment it is sent.

Who is affected: sites with load balancing, mostly with a home battery that charges from the grid or with a charger that accepts a new current only every few seconds. Other sites are not changed.

Nothing needs doing after the update.
