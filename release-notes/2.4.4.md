SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.4.4 finds a BMW's plug sensor from BMW CarData.

## Fixes

- **A car's plug sensor whose name says "charging port" is found again.** SpotNav left out every sensor of a car with "port" in its name, so that a charge-port door would not be taken for the plug. That also left out the plug sensor of the BavarianData integration (BMW CarData), "Charging Port plug state", and the car's plug sensor showed as missing. A sensor that names both the port and the plug now counts; a port door still does not. Among BMW's plug sensors the AC one is chosen, before the combined connection state and the DC inlet's.

## Improved

- **A sharper icon.** SpotNav's icon now comes in 512 px too, for high-resolution screens and Home Assistant's Marketplace.
