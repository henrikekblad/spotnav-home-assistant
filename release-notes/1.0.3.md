SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.0.3 fixes the site setup form and adds more ways to measure a charger's current.

## Fixes

- **"Automatic" no longer fails with "Unknown error occurred" when adding a charger.** Listing the devices failed on installations where another integration registers a device with an unusual identifier.
- **Every field in the site setup now has a proper label.** The charger wiring (number of phases, connected phase and measured current) used to show internal names. Each charger now has its own step with a translated form, both when creating a site and when editing it.
- **Sensors without a device class can be used for the site measurement.** Meter readers built with ESPHome and similar often state no device class. Any sensor can now be picked, and its unit is checked when you continue: A or mA for current, W or kW for power, V for voltage, var or kvar for reactive power, VA or kVA for apparent power.

## New

- **A charger's current from three separate entities.** Integrations such as Charge Amps provide one current sensor per phase. You can now pick those three entities as the charger's measured current, and SpotNav suggests them automatically when it recognises the charger.
