SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.5.0 lets the hours before you installed it lift the import target, and holds a Zaptec under it despite its slow current writes.

## Improved

- **In the month you install SpotNav, the hours before it measured can lift the import target.** The month's peak lifts the import target for the rest of the month, but until now only hours SpotNav measured itself counted: hours filled in from Home Assistant's statistics never did, since an hourly mean of a sensor that was unavailable part of the hour may be higher than what the meter billed. Now an hour of the running month counts like a measured one when Home Assistant's 5-minute statistics have all twelve 5-minute rows of it for every grid power sensor (they are kept ten days by default), at the mean of those twelve rows. A site whose peak this month was already 6.4 kW before the install is no longer held under its 6.0 kW target for the rest of the month. Other hours from the statistics still never lift the target.
- **The import target holds a Zaptec, which takes a new current only every 15 minutes, by energy.** Until its next write the charger keeps drawing what it draws now, so a lowering is now made deep enough to make up for that wait, and where no current can bring the hour home in time (late in the hour) the charge is paused instead, as a pause goes out within seconds. In the last 16 minutes of the hour it is kept where it is set, so no raise reaches into the next hour. Such hours may give the car a little less than a charger that takes a new current at once.
