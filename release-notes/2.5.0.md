SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.5.0 lets the hours before you installed it lift the import target, where Home Assistant's statistics show they are complete.

## Improved

- **In the month you install SpotNav, the hours before it measured can lift the import target.** The month's peak lifts the import target for the rest of the month, but until now only hours SpotNav measured itself counted: hours filled in from Home Assistant's statistics never did, since an hourly mean of a sensor that was unavailable part of the hour may be higher than what the meter billed. Now an hour of the running month counts like a measured one when Home Assistant's 5-minute statistics have every reading of it for the grid power (they are kept ten days by default), at the mean of those readings. A site whose peak this month was already 6.4 kW before the install is no longer held under its 6.0 kW target for the rest of the month. Other hours from the statistics still never lift the target.
