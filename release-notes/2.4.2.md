SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.4.2 leaves the reload to you after an update, and gives a removed charger back as SpotNav found it.

## Changed

- **The card no longer reloads the page by itself after an update.** When your browser or the Companion app still runs the card from before an update, the card shows **SpotNav has been updated** with a **Reload** button, and the page reloads when you press it.
- **A charger you remove from SpotNav gets back the current it had before SpotNav.** Just before SpotNav first sets a charger's current, it now notes the current the charger had. When you remove the charger, SpotNav sets that current again; if SpotNav did not know it (it could not be read, or the charger was added before this version), the current is left as it is. SpotNav turns the charger back on only if SpotNav itself had stopped it; a stop you made, in the card, the app or elsewhere, is left as it is.
