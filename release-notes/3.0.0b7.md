SpotNav for Home Assistant 3.0.0b7 is the seventh beta of 3.0. **Learns your home. Plans your energy.**

This is still a beta, offered only to those who switch on beta versions in HACS (or the Marketplace). 2.5.x stays the normal release. Everything new that could write to a battery, an inverter or a car still starts by only watching, or behind its own opt-in, as in the earlier betas.

## What is new

- **Any Octopus Energy tariff, and your export rate as the sell price.** In Great Britain SpotNav now reads every import tariff the Octopus Energy integration holds rates for (Flux, Go, Cosy and fixed tariffs as well as Agile), and prices each sold kWh at your export meter's own rate (Flux export, Agile Outgoing or fixed Outgoing) where the integration has an export meter. See [Prices](docs/prices.md).
- **An update no longer pauses battery control.** After a firmware, integration or SpotNav update, control keeps running on the last check that passed while the battery is checked again, at any hour. Steps the plan itself shows on the new version count towards that check (a hold, a grid charge or covering the house that the battery is seen to follow), and each step keeps the latest answer it has from an earlier version, so a version that showed only some steps leaves no step off. What is still not shown after seven days is checked ahead of the plan at the next suitable moment, for at most five minutes. See [Home batteries](docs/home-battery.md).
- **A clearer hardware test.** A test that failed nothing but could not show everything the control needs now says **Not complete** and names what is missing, instead of "passed". A step that had no room under the fuse no longer stops the rest; the inverter's own timeout is measured right after the hold; a pressed test waits until its small grid charge fits under the fuse; and the charge is judged at the rate SpotNav could actually ask for. Batteries whose rating SpotNav cannot read (EcoFlow, Marstek, Kostal, Ferroamp, Tesla) can now pass it.
- **Three-phase Solis hybrids** are counted as balanced over three phases (read from the inverter's own type), so a grid charge fits on a normal main fuse.
- **Shorter guide pages.** The large pages of the guide start with a short summary, the home battery page with a table of brands; brand details, the grid charge cap and the load balancing rules have pages of their own.

## Fixes

- **Disabling a charger in SpotNav** (Settings → Devices & services) now hands it back as removing it does, and it is left out of load balancing until it is enabled again. Disabling the whole site gives back a charge load balancing held.
- **Phase switching** waits while a charger's status cannot be read or the car is drawing, on chargers that may only switch before a start.
- **A hold right after a grid charge** raises its charge limit at once, so the sun is no longer exported for up to a minute.
- **Removing a site** with a pool, spa or sauna still held says what to set back by hand.
- **A battery mirrored through Remote Home-Assistant** is no longer given a brand that only shared entity names suggested.

## What to do after updating

- **Battery testers:** if your hardware test said "passed" but no control code came, run it again: it now says what is missing and waits for a moment it fits.
- **Great Britain:** enable the Octopus Energy integration's export rate entities if you want your export rate used.
- **After the update, reload the card:** hard-refresh the browser (Ctrl+Shift+R). In Home Assistant's companion app, reset its frontend cache (Android: Settings → Companion app → Troubleshooting → Reset frontend cache; iPhone and iPad: Settings → Companion app → Debugging → Reset frontend cache), then close the app fully and open it again. See [Troubleshooting](docs/troubleshooting.md).

## Known limits

- Of the battery recipes, only Sigenergy has run under control on a real plant; Solis has passed the hold and hand-back on a real plant. Every other brand waits for its first tester.
- Octopus tariffs other than Agile and the export rates are untested on a real account.
- The SpotNav app shows the battery's tester stage but has no sign-up or code entry: those are done in Home Assistant by an administrator.

## Before you downgrade

Going back to 3.0.0b6 works: what 3.0.0b7 stores is additive. On 3.0.0b6 a hardware test kept as "not complete" reads as not passed, the export rate is not used, and battery control pauses again after an update until its check has run.

## How to get the beta

1. **Make a Home Assistant backup first** (Settings → System → Backups).
2. In HACS, open SpotNav, the menu (three dots), *Redownload*, switch on *Show beta versions* and choose `3.0.0b7`.
3. Restart Home Assistant, then hard-refresh the browser (or reset the app's frontend cache).

Already on an earlier 3.0 beta? *Update* is enough. Going back to 2.5.x: use **2.5.5 or later**, as described in the 3.0.0b1 notes.
