SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.22.0 keeps your price area, VAT, energy tax and grid fee in one place for every charger, and makes the site's settings page shorter and plainer.

## One electricity price for every charger

- **Area and fees are set once.** The price area, VAT, energy tax and grid fee now belong to an **Electricity price** device, not to each charger. A site uses one electricity price and all its chargers follow it; a charger without a site uses one itself. Change the area or a fee in the card, on the device or under its **Configure**, and every charger that uses it plans again with the new figures. The card and the app change the same electricity price.
- **Made for you.** The first charger or site you add makes the electricity price from Home Assistant's country and location, with the area's suggested fees. No dialog asks for the area any more; chargers and sites added later use the same one.
- **The card shows where the price lives.** The **Electricity price** section names the price and how many chargers use it, for example *Electricity price SE4 · used by all 2 chargers*, and says that a change there applies to every charger using it.
- **Several prices when you need them.** A second address or a charger with other fees (a company charger without VAT, for example) can have an electricity price of its own. With more than one, the section has a **Price from** row where an administrator picks which one the charger uses; on a site, the choice applies to every charger on it.
- **Upgrading.** On the first start, once, each charger's area and fees move to an electricity price. Chargers with the same area and fees share one, and an existing electricity price with exactly those fees is used. Nothing is changed in silence: when an existing electricity price has the same area but other fees, the chargers get one of their own and the card asks once, *Two electricity prices for SE4 with different fees. Use one?* **Use …** moves every charger and site to the one you pick; the other is kept, and can be removed under **Devices & services**. **Keep both** leaves them as they are. Past charges keep the prices they had.
- **Entities moved.** The charger's entities for the price area, the three fee policies and the VAT, energy tax and grid transfer fee are removed; the electricity price device has them. An automation that used the charger's entities needs the electricity price's instead.

## Site settings

- **Shorter and plainer.** Every field on the site's settings page has a plain name and a line on what it does. The card's site switch and the settings page both call it **Load balancing**, and the switch **Next: change meter and phases** comes last.
- **Fewer settings.** The switch for the site's calculation is gone: a site always calculates once it has a measurement, also one that had it off. The load-balancing settings *smallest change* and *wait before a change* and yield stepping's *hard ceiling* are gone too; they are fixed at their former defaults of 2 A, 60 seconds and 1.15 times the main fuse, whatever a site had stored.
