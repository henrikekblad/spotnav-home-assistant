SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.15.1 stops starting a full car again while load balancing holds the charge back, and can wake a car to read its level when a charge ends.

## Load balancing

- **A full car is not started again and again.** While a home battery charged from the grid and filled the main fuse, load balancing paused the plan's charge, and every ten to fifteen minutes SpotNav started the car at its minimum current to see whether the battery would give way. A car that was already full took nothing, and the next try came all the same, for as long as the battery charged. Now a try counts as refused when the charger itself reports that the car took nothing: at most 1 A on every phase it uses, never charging, and the car declining the charge (*SuspendedEV*). After two refused tries in a row the car has ended the charge: no more tries and no resume start the car, and the plan waits for the car's level, as it does when the car ends the charge with current offered. A fresh reading below the target, unplugging, raising the target or a person's Start ends the wait. A try the battery did not give way to, or one where the car drew, is not counted.

## Target charge level

- **Wake the car for a reading.** Many car integrations answer a re-read from their cloud's copy of the car, and a sleeping car's copy can be hours old. A new car setting, **Wake the car for a reading** (off by default, set by an administrator), lets SpotNav ask the car itself to report when a charge ends and the ordinary re-read brought nothing fresh: when only an estimate says the target is reached, and when the car ended the plan's charge and the plan waits for its level. It uses the car's 12 V battery and a request from the maker's limit, so SpotNav wakes a car at most once per charge end and never within 30 minutes of its last wake, also across a restart. The setting is shown for cars whose integration can wake them: Kia and Hyundai (*Kia Uvo / Hyundai Bluelink*, which asks every car on the account to report) and Tesla (*Tesla Fleet*, *Teslemetry*, *Tessie*). A wake that fails changes nothing else; the last one is shown in the diagnostics.
