SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.2.0 brings solar and hybrid charging to sites whose meter reports current per phase, such as the Tibber Pulse.

## New

- **Solar and hybrid with a current-per-phase meter.** Meters like the Tibber Pulse report each phase's current but not its direction, so SpotNav could not see a solar surplus and offered only the cheapest-hours strategy. A site can now add the meter's total grid power ("Total grid power (for solar)" in the site's measurement settings), either one sensor or import and export as two. SpotNav finds it by itself for Tibber, DSMR, DSMR Reader, P1 Monitor, HomeWizard and Shelly Pro 3EM. With it, Solar and Hybrid become available: the surplus is the total export, shared over the phases the charger uses and kept within each phase's fuse headroom. Fuse protection and load balancing still use the per-phase currents.
- **A clear reason when solar is not available.** Without the total, the strategy dialog says that solar needs the meter's total grid power and links to the site settings.
