SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.18.0 lets SpotNav's new charge-ownership logic decide for every charger, gives other automations SpotNav's electricity price, makes the estimate of the car's level more accurate, and sends a command the charger could not take after a restart once it can.

## Charging decisions

- **The new charge-ownership logic decides.** Which charge belongs to the plan, the sun, a person or the charger itself, and so when SpotNav starts and stops a charger, is now decided by the new logic for every charger. It has run beside the previous logic since 6 October without a single disagreement. The previous logic still runs alongside and is compared, so the debug info shows any difference. A charge running during the update carries on; nothing is stopped or restarted.
- **A way back per charger.** If a charger misbehaves, an administrator can switch just that charger back: Settings → Devices & services → SpotNav → the charger → **Configure** → **Charging decisions** → **Previous logic**. Please open an issue with the debug info if you need to.
- **A command after a restart is not lost.** Right after Home Assistant started, before the charger's own integration had loaded, a stop (or start, or current) SpotNav decided could be counted as sent while nothing reached the charger, which then charged on its own until the next planned period. Now such a command waits for the charger, at most 15 minutes, and SpotNav decides again on what holds once the charger is there: a period that opened meanwhile starts it rather than stopping it.

## Electricity price for other automations

- **A new entry, "An electricity price".** Add it from SpotNav's **Add entry** to use SpotNav's prices for things that have nothing to do with a charger, such as floor heating or a washing machine. Choose the price area and the VAT, energy tax and grid fee, as for a charger. It gives:
  - **Price now**: the current quarter's price with fees and VAT, in kr/kWh (or the area's unit).
  - **Cheap now** and **Expensive now**: on during the day's cheapest and most expensive hours, as many as **Cheap hours per day** (default 6) and **Expensive hours per day** (default 4) say.
  - The action **spotnav.get_prices**: today's and tomorrow's prices with fees, shaped like Home Assistant's own Nord Pool integration.
  The hours are ranked per calendar day and quarter by quarter, not as one unbroken period. The guide [Prices for other automations](https://github.com/henrikekblad/spotnav-home-assistant/blob/main/docs/price-device.md) has a floor-heating and a washing-machine example. Several entries are allowed, and chargers are unaffected.

## Target charge level

- **The estimate counts from when the car measured.** A car's integration often delivers its level minutes after the car measured it, and the energy charged in between was not counted, so the estimate ran a few points behind the car (on 22 October it said 80 % when the car was at 83 %). When the car's device has a sensor with the time the car reported (Kia, Hyundai and others) and the charger's meter reported steadily then, the estimate now counts from that time.
- **…but only when the readings agree.** That time is used only when the change in level between two readings agrees with the energy the charger delivered. A car whose time does not (on 25 October a reading said 53 % then 60 % with nothing charged in between) is counted the careful way until a later pair of readings agrees.
