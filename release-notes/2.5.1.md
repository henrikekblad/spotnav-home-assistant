SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.5.1 lets the car take the home battery's share again while the battery holds the grid at the fuse.

## Fixes

- **With *car first*, a car charging below its plan steps back into the share of a home battery that charges from the grid and holds the grid at the fuse.** The car was not lowered for such a battery, but it was not raised either: a battery that had not yet been seen to give way kept the main fuse as the ceiling of every step, and a grid held at 20.07 A on a 20 A fuse never left room for the step that would show it. The car could stay at 9 A all evening under a battery charging 6.7 kW. Now one 2 A step goes into the battery's share, at most 2.5 A over the limit on any phase; if the battery gives way the car climbs on to its plan, and if it does not, the step is taken back at once and not tried again for 10 minutes, then 20, 40 and at most an hour.

## Improved

- **Save and Cancel stay at the bottom of the card's dialogs, so a long dialog such as the plan's no longer has to be scrolled to the end to save.**
