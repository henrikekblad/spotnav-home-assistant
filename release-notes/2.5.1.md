SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.5.1 lets the car take the home battery's share again while the battery holds the grid at the fuse.

## Fixes

- **With *car first*, a car charging below its plan steps back into the share of a home battery that charges from the grid and holds the grid at the fuse.** The car was not lowered for such a battery, but it was not raised either: a battery that had not yet been seen to give way kept the main fuse as the ceiling of every step, and a grid held at 20.07 A on a 20 A fuse never left room for the step that would show it. The car could stay at 9 A all evening under a battery charging 6.7 kW. Now one 2 A step goes into the battery's share, at most 2.5 A over the limit on any phase; if the battery gives way the car climbs on to its plan, and if it does not, the step is taken back at once and not tried again for 10 minutes, then 20, 40 and at most an hour.
- **Resume hands the charge back to the plan.** After a Start, Resume let the charge run on as the person's even outside every window of the plan, and the plan's own charge then needed Stop and Resume once more. Resume now ends the Start: outside the plan's windows the charge stops, with no notification, and the plan starts at its next window; inside an open window it goes on as the plan's own charge. A Stop followed by Resume works as before.
- **A charge running when Home Assistant restarts is no longer left without control.** The first calculation after a restart ran before the charger's saved state was read back, so it installed a new plan and saved it over that state: the charge lost its owner and its requested current, and load balancing then had nothing to regulate it by. The saved state is now read first, so the plan in force is kept and a window that is charging runs to its end. A charge outside the plan's windows is stopped once the charger can be commanded, and a stop the charger did not take (an OCPP charger right after a restart, whose integration does not know the transaction yet) is sent again two minutes later. A charge with no current on record is regulated at the charging current set, the plan's, or 16 A.
