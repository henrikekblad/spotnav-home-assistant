SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.5.3 stops warning that the car will not be ready when the plan falls short by only a sliver.

## New

- **A blueprint for a heat pump's relay: Relay by electricity price.** A relay (a Shelly or any switch) on a heat pump's potential-free input is closed while electricity is expensive (heating curve lowered or an add-on heater blocked, with a longest closed time, a rest after it and an outdoor temperature floor), pulsed when it is cheap (extra hot water, at most a set number of times a day), or drives the two SG Ready inputs. Shortest closing (45 minutes, 0 turns it off) keeps a single expensive quarter-hour from closing the relay: it closes only when the expensive stretch ahead lasts at least that long, and then stays closed until Expensive now ends. SpotNav installs it with the other two, under the card's Settings, Support, Automations.

## Fixes

- **No "will not be ready by the departure" when the plan holds all but a sliver of the need.** A plan is made of whole quarter-hours; when the last one needed would start just after the departure, the plan said "Will not be ready by the departure: about 40.2 of 40.2 kWh by 13:00" although the car would be done by about 12:40. A plan that falls short by at most one percent of the need now counts as ready; every quarter-hour up to the departure is still planned, and a real shortfall is still told as before.
- **A charger's settings stored by a later SpotNav with a departure calendar now load in this release.** Going back from a 3.0 beta kept the charger's area, current, departure and target at their defaults until saved again; now the record loads, and the calendar is kept unused here and still there after a save, for when 3.0 is installed again.
- **No more charge-limit question about a presence sensor, and the question can be dismissed.** An mmWave presence sensor reports a distance and percent settings, which looked like a car's range and two charge limits, so Repairs asked "A vehicle's charge limit needs a decision" about it and offered no way out. A charge limit is now asked about only on a device that also reports a state of charge, the leftover question goes away by itself, and the charge-limit question now offers "This is not a vehicle" like the state-of-charge one.
- **A battery-powered presence sensor is no longer taken for a vehicle.** A presence sensor with a battery reports a battery percent and a distance in centimetres or metres, which looked like a car's state of charge and range. A range now counts only in kilometres or miles.
