SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.1.0 helps a new installation get going, says more about a plan, balances chargers that can only be switched on and off, and finds more hardware.

## New

- **Get started.** While something is left to set up, the card's attention line says *Get started: 3 things left* to an administrator, and its list has one row per thing (your car, its charge level, a site with the grid meter, a solar forecast, phase switching for solar, phones for notifications, the SpotNav app), each with a button to where it is done; the optional ones can be answered *Not needed*, and the line goes when nothing is left.
- **What a plan saves, and when the sun comes.** The status line adds *saves ~18 kr against charging now* to a plan that is at least 5 % cheaper than charging the same energy right away (with your fees and VAT, until the charge starts), Hybrid's *kWh expected from sun* gains its hours, and the Solar strategy says how much sun the forecast promises for today or tomorrow, and when.
- **Blueprints for the rest of the house.** *Setpoint by electricity price* gives floor heating, radiators or a heat pump a higher temperature when electricity is cheap and a lower one when it is expensive (with an optional max price and away temperature), and *Run on solar surplus* switches a pool heater, water heater or immersion heater on when a site's solar surplus is large enough and off when it is gone. SpotNav installs both in Home Assistant and keeps them up to date (a changed copy is overwritten; copy it to another folder to keep your own). An administrator finds them under *Card settings*, *Support*, *Automations*, with how many automations use each and a button to Home Assistant's Blueprints page; see [Automations](https://github.com/henrikekblad/spotnav-home-assistant/blob/main/docs/automations.md).

## Load balancing and solar

- **Chargers that can only be switched on and off are balanced and follow the sun.** A charger whose current SpotNav cannot set (Monta, myenergi zappi, Ohme, go-e legacy, ChargePoint, a charger behind a smart plug) is now reckoned at the current it draws, learned from its own current sensors or the plug's power, else from the site meter's step when SpotNav switches it, else the charging current you set. Load balancing pauses it whole when a phase is over the fuse (after lowering the chargers whose current can be set) and resumes it once there is room for all of it; before, it was never paused, and a site of only such chargers had no fuse protection. Solar starts it only on a surplus for its whole current and stops it when the surplus falls below that. The card says load balancing can only pause its charge and no longer shows it as limited to a current.

## More hardware

- **Solar and hybrid work on a Ferroamp site.** SpotNav now takes the EnergyHub's total grid power (*Grid Power*) along with its phase currents when it finds the meter, so a Ferroamp site has the signed grid power that solar and hybrid need. Import counts as positive, as the EnergyHub reports it. An existing Ferroamp site can pick *Grid Power* as its total grid power in the site editor.
- **More home batteries are found when you set up a site:** Ferroamp (the EnergyHub's *Battery Power*), Zendure SolarFlow, Hyper and AIO, Anker Solix Solarbanks (the system's total when it has one), EcoFlow PowerStream and Ocean Pro, and Marstek through its local API (the system's total when there are several). SpotNav only reads a battery's power, to tell the car's draw from the battery's; it never controls the battery.
- **NexBlue chargers can be started and stopped.** With Home Assistant 2026.10 or later, SpotNav starts and stops a NexBlue through the integration's *Charging* switch and reads whether it charges from its *Charging state* sensor. The integration cannot set the current, so the charger is switched on and off whole, like the other chargers above, at the current SpotNav learns from its phase currents. With an older Home Assistant, which has no such switch, a NexBlue is still recognised as read only.

## Fixes

- **The debug file now keeps the last 24 hours and the last five charging sessions, also across restarts and updates.** *Download debug info* has one row a minute for the whole day (with what solar decided and the phases each charger is on), each charger's last five plug-ins with what solar, load balancing, the phase switching and a person did in each, and the last day's warnings, so a file downloaded in the evening still shows what happened in the afternoon.
- **When load balancing has to cut, a charger with priority First now keeps its current.** The cut is taken from Last first, then Normal, as documented; it was the other way round.
- **A plan made for settings you have changed no longer runs when the new answer is to wait.** Changing the departure or the need while SpotNav then waits for prices (or for a cheaper night it expects) left the old plan in place, so it started at its old time. The old plan is now cleared and the status shows the wait; a window already charging still runs to its end.
- **A home battery that charges from the grid no longer pauses the car for the night.** With *the battery gives way* on, a battery that holds the grid at the fuse (a Sigenergy, say) lowered the car amp by amp and paused it, and the pause could then last until the plan's window ended: an OCPP charger such as the Charge Amps HALO keeps showing its last current while stopped, which SpotNav took for a car still charging, and a charger that reported its charge once more as it stopped made SpotNav forget the pause altogether (and say the charge *did not start as planned*). Now the car is held at its minimum while SpotNav checks that the battery gives way, then climbs back as it does; a battery that does not give way still has the car paused at once, and a paused charge is resumed as soon as there is room.
