SpotNav for Home Assistant 3.0.0b5 is the fifth beta of 3.0. **Learns your home. Plans your energy.**

This is still a beta, offered only to those who switch on beta versions in HACS (or the Marketplace). 2.5.x stays the normal release. Everything new that could write to a pool, a spa, a sauna, a battery or a heat pump still starts by only watching, or behind its own opt-in, as in the earlier betas.

## What is new

- **Pool, spa and sauna.** SpotNav now plans a pool (its circulation pump and its heat pump), a spa and a sauna as loads that can be moved: filtering and pool heat in the sunniest or cheapest hours, the spa and the sauna warm by the times you book. It **only watches first** and shows what it would do. Nothing is written until an administrator turns on the load's own switch (**Control the circulation**, **Control the pool heat pump**, **Control the spa**, **Control the sauna**). Supported routes include Tuya Local, core Tuya, Aquatemp, WarmLink, Fairland, Poolex, PoolSync, iAquaLink and ScreenLogic for pool heat pumps; Balboa, Bestway, Gecko, Intex, MSpa and SmartTub for spas; Harvia, Huum, Saunum, Tylö and Sentio for saunas; and any climate entity or switch you choose. **None of the control has run on real equipment yet:** a Madimack pool heat pump through Tuya Local with a relay for the circulation, and a Balboa spa, are being tried now. Every other route is untested; testers are welcome. See [Pool, spa and sauna](docs/pool-spa.md) and [Supported devices](docs/supported.md#supported-pool-heat-pumps-spas-and-saunas).
- **Pool, Spa and Sauna tabs, and a settings section for each.** The heat card has a tab for each load once it is set up or found, with the dial, when it is ready, a button to heat now and the next hours. The gear opens at the section of the shown tab. Pool heat pumps, spas and saunas are left out of the heat zone and setpoint choices.
- **One priority list.** One list per site, **Priority** in the settings, replaces the solar priority and the charger priority: house heat, the cars, the spa and sauna, the water heater, the home battery, the spa between baths, the pool's circulation and heat pump. The top gets energy first and gives way last. Until you save the list, your old settings count as before. An older app keeps working: its change of a charger's priority moves the car in the list and it reads back what it set. See [The priority list](docs/site-and-load-balancing.md#the-priority-list).
- **Not ready in time, across cars, baths and sauna sessions.** When more than one car's charge, or a booked bath or sauna session, will not be ready in time, each phone gets **one** notice for the whole site instead of one per load, with the choices that free the most as buttons. A pool that has been cold for days in autumn asks once a season if you want to close the pool season.
- **Notifications per phone.** Every card's settings have one **Notifications** section for the home, listing the phones. Open a phone to choose whether it gets anything, which events it hears about (Charging, Pool, Spa, Sauna) and which chargers it follows. A phone with both the SpotNav app and the Home Assistant app is one entry and is told through one route, never twice. On the first start of 3.0 your earlier choices are carried over, so nobody loses a notice they get today. See [Notifications](docs/notifications.md).
- **Charging on after a missed departure.** If the departure passes with the car still short of its target and still plugged in, SpotNav now charges on at once instead of waiting for the next departure's cheap hours, until the need is met, the car is unplugged, you press Stop, or three hours have passed. See [Strategies](docs/strategies.md).
- **A car whose charge level cannot be read.** SpotNav plans from its estimate, or from the last reading while it is under a day old, or else a full charge to the target. The card says so calmly after 15 minutes, and when the car's own integration needs you to sign in again it names that integration and offers a button to Devices & services. This is also in 2.5.8.
- **More home batteries are read.** Read only, from the integrations' sources and not yet tested on a battery: sonnen, Alpha ESS, E3/DC, Growatt (0xAHA), SENEC, SAJ, SMA, GivEnergy, EcoFlow PowerOcean, Kostal, Fronius, Homevolt, Deye and Sunsynk, Anker Solix, Zendure, Omnibattery, Sungrow and Marstek. SpotNav writes nothing to them. See [Home batteries](docs/home-battery.md).
- **The pool, spa and sauna in the plan and the flow.** They have their own layers in the Plan tab and their power is learned. In the house card's flow, **Heat** now counts them with the house's heat, in its pie.
- **Safer battery control.** A battery's guard against Home Assistant stopping is now trusted only where SpotNav can renew it and has measured it on that firmware, and power the battery itself pulls from the grid counts everywhere against the fuse.

## Fixes

- **Load balancing's safety stop:** a plan's charge that was stopped for safety is started again when there is room, after a wait that grows with each stop in the same plug-in.
- **The sun's value** is also shown on a site without a home battery, and as a part of the Earnings tab.
- **Marstek, SolarEdge and Omnibattery** are read and recognised more reliably.
- **Several fixes to the new loads** from the review before this beta: a season close that hands back, the spa's frost floor and its temperature between baths, sauna sessions booked through Later, and phones joined by name or model told only once.

## What to do after updating

- **Pool, spa or sauna owners:** open the heat card's tabs and the gear's sections, check what SpotNav found, and leave the control switches off until the plan looks right. Report how your equipment behaves.
- **Open Settings → Notifications** and check each phone's route and events.
- **Open Settings → Priority** and save the list if you want a different order than the default.
- **After the update, reload the card:** hard-refresh the browser (Ctrl+Shift+R). In Home Assistant's companion app, reset its frontend cache (Android: Settings → Companion app → Troubleshooting → Reset frontend cache; iPhone and iPad: Settings → Companion app → Debugging → Reset frontend cache), then close the app fully and open it again. See [Troubleshooting](docs/troubleshooting.md).

## Known limits

- Pool, spa and sauna control has not run on real equipment yet. A Madimack pool heat pump (Tuya Local, with a relay for the circulation) and a Balboa spa are being tried; everything else is untested, and some spas and saunas are only watched.
- The new battery brands are read only; there is no control for them.
- The SpotNav app does not yet draw everything new; an older app ignores the new fields and shows no spa, sauna or pool notice (those go to the phone's Home Assistant app).

## Before you downgrade

Going back to 3.0.0b4 or 2.5.x: pool, spa and sauna settings and the per-phone notification settings are not read by 3.0.0b4, so they are ignored there (your charger notification choices from before are kept). The priority list also writes the old solar and charger priority settings, so the order you set is kept.

## How to get the beta

1. **Make a Home Assistant backup first** (Settings → System → Backups).
2. In HACS, open SpotNav, the menu (three dots), *Redownload*, switch on *Show beta versions* and choose `3.0.0b5`.
3. Restart Home Assistant, then hard-refresh the browser (or reset the app's frontend cache).

Already on an earlier 3.0 beta? *Update* is enough. Going back to 2.5.x: use **2.5.5 or later**, as described in the 3.0.0b1 notes.
