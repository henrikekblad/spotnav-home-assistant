SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.14.1 plans as soon as tomorrow's prices arrive, also with the car unplugged.

## Charger control

- **Tomorrow's prices replan at once.** After a setting had been changed (a departure set, say), the plan no longer heard of new prices: when tomorrow's prices arrived it kept saying *Waiting for tomorrow's prices* until something else made it plan again, often plugging the car in hours later. The plan is now worked out again as soon as the prices arrive, whether a car is plugged in or not, so the card, the app and the plan's sensors show the plan before the car comes home.
