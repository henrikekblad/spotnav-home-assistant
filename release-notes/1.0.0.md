SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.0.0 is the first public release. It charges your EV when electricity is cheapest, or from solar surplus, and keeps the charging current under your main fuse.

## Highlights

- **Planning on day-ahead prices** for the Nordic and Baltic countries, Germany and the Netherlands. Plans use published prices only: when tomorrow's prices are not out yet, SpotNav waits for them and buys now only what cannot wait for your departure.
- **Strategies:** cheapest, solar surplus and hybrid (solar forecast plus prices), with a departure time and an optional target state of charge.
- **Load balancing** under the main fuse from your grid meter, with an admin switch for active control.
- **Automatic setup:** chargers (OCPP and many charger integrations), grid meters, house batteries and vehicles are detected from their devices, with a confirm step instead of long forms. New chargers start with a price area, phases and current suggested from your Home Assistant location and charger.
- **A bundled Lovelace card** with the price chart, the plan, Start/Stop/Pause and all settings, and pairing for the SpotNav Android app.

## Install

In HACS, add this repository as a custom repository (type Integration), install SpotNav and restart Home Assistant. Then add the integration under Settings → Devices & services. See the [setup guide](https://github.com/henrikekblad/spotnav-home-assistant/blob/main/docs/setup.md) and the [supported hardware](https://github.com/henrikekblad/spotnav-home-assistant/blob/main/docs/supported.md).

Requires Home Assistant 2026.9.0 or newer.

## Upgrading from 0.x

Versions 0.x used the domain `elpris_charging`. This release uses `spotnav` and cannot take over the old configuration: remove the old integration and its entries, then add SpotNav again.
