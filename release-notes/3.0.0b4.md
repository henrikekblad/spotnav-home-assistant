SpotNav for Home Assistant 3.0.0b4 is the fourth beta of 3.0. **Learns your home. Plans your energy.**

This is still a beta, offered only to those who switch on beta versions in HACS (or the Marketplace). 2.5.x stays the normal release. Everything new that could write to a battery or a heat pump still starts in **shadow** or behind its own opt-in, as in the earlier betas.

## What is new

- **The Plan tab steps back in time.** The house card's Plan tab now goes back a day at a time: what was planned at each hour's start, what actually ran and why, and today with a line at the present hour. SpotNav keeps a compact record per site of each hour's plan and what happened, and the debug info carries the last days of it. See [The house card](docs/house-card.md#plan).
- **Sigenergy on a small main fuse: one tap.** The battery settings' import-limit line now has a button. **Set 13.1 kW…** lowers the plant's own grid import limit to what keeps every phase under the fuse (3 × (fuse − safety margin) × voltage), reads it back, and only then turns on *The import limit acts*. **Turn on…** appears when the limit is already low enough. SpotNav never raises the limit and writes nothing while battery control is off. Where this way is open, *If Home Assistant stops* is no longer offered: the plant guards the fuse without accepting a risk. See [Grid charging on a small main fuse](docs/home-battery.md#grid-charging-on-a-small-main-fuse-sigenergy).
- **The battery's wear cost is a setting.** The battery's **Mode** row now opens a dialog with each mode explained, Balanced's selling margin, a link to the battery plan and a **Wear** field: what charging and discharging one kWh costs in wear, each way, in your currency's minor unit (öre, øre, cent, p…). Left empty it is the default (15 öre/kWh in Sweden); Balanced still counts it twice. The plan, its nightly review and the comparison use it. The settings page itself shows only the mode. See [The battery plan](docs/battery-plan.md).
- **Solis batteries are read** through the `solis_modbus` integration: the level, the power, the charge and discharge ratings (the BMS's current limits × its voltage, never above the inverter's rated output), the floor, the storage mode and remote-control switches (so SpotNav can tell whether the inverter runs itself), the firmware and the lifetime counters. The size is learnt. Read only: there is no Solis control yet. See [Home batteries](docs/home-battery.md).
- **Batteries whose integration enables little by default.** The battery's opt-in now also enables the entities SpotNav reads (for Sigenergy the grid sensor status and output type), and a size or floor that sits in a disabled entity shows as one line with a button that turns just those on. A battery planner that is missing right after a start (Predbat) gets ten minutes before an outside-control verdict ends, and SpotNav writes nothing to the battery in that time. Predbat in read-only mode counts as only watching once its switch has been on for ten minutes.
- **Settings, Support, the same in every card.** The charging card and the house and heat cards now draw Support the same way: Automations, **Beta tester** and **Hardware test** as rows, then rows to act on: **Documentation** (SpotNav's site), About this card, download and send debug info, disconnect all phones, and **Support the SpotNav project**. The hardware test has its own dialog with the last result and its day; *Send the result to the developer* sits under the result. The e-mail address is one per installation: given once, in Beta tester or with *Send debug info*, it is filled in the next time.
- **Get started in the charging card** now also shows the site's home battery and heating rows, and opens Settings at the top. *Not needed* holds in every card.
- **Settings is easier to move around.** The cross, Escape or a tap outside an editor goes back to Settings, as Cancel does, also from About this card and Automations. On a wide screen Settings and its editors are wider, so long values stay on one line.

## Fixes

- **A car that finishes its charge itself on an Easee** (the charger says *completed*) was taken for a charger stopped by something else: the plan lost its charge, an outside pause was counted, and a later window still started the full car. SpotNav now sees the car's own end, and a car read as full ends the plan.
- **The battery plan:** a grid charge is planned under the same cap that control obeys (none on a cap of zero or one nobody knows, never below the least rate, never where control runs none), and the battery lane says when the fuse held charging back. The plan's words give the power an action really ran at and for how long. The learnt round trip the plan uses is capped at 95 %.
- **Ratings from a BMS's current limit** (GoodWe, Solinteg, FoxESS, Victron, SolaX, Solis): the BMS lowers its limit near full or empty and in the cold, so a higher power the battery has been seen to hold now counts instead. A rating the plant itself states is never replaced. SolaX's ratings are now read from its BMS currents.
- **Victron:** never a discharge while the GX's *Max inverter power* is 0 (the checklist says so); a grid setpoint found far below 0 is handed back as the GX's default 50 W and the checklist tells you, a small bias is kept; controls that read unknown are not taken as failures.
- **SolarEdge:** the command timeout is written again at every change of action, so the inverter no longer falls back mid-plan.
- **Writes and renewals:** an action that flips at every look is counted and stops at the day's cap; a renewal owed is no longer skipped for half an hour.
- **Easee circuits:** the circuit's dynamic limit is read (never written) and shown as a status line while it caps a charge below what SpotNav set.
- **A car's or a phone's battery** is never read as the house battery.
- **The hardware test** says it cannot run right now when a part is there but busy, instead of asking to turn control on.

## What to do after updating

- **Open the house card's Plan tab** and step back a day to see what ran and why (the record starts with this update).
- **Sigenergy on a 16–20 A fuse:** in the battery settings, press **Set … kW** or **Turn on…** on the import limit's line. If you accepted *If Home Assistant stops* earlier, you can turn it off.
- **Solis owners:** turn off *essential sensors only* in the `solis_modbus` integration so the storage mode and switches exist, and check that the BMS voltage and current-limit sensors are enabled.
- **After the update, reload the card:** hard-refresh the browser (Ctrl+Shift+R). In Home Assistant's companion app, reset its frontend cache (Android: Settings → Companion app → Troubleshooting → Reset frontend cache; iPhone and iPad: Settings → Companion app → Debugging → Reset frontend cache), then close the app fully and open it again. See [Troubleshooting](docs/troubleshooting.md).

## Known limits

- Solis is read only; control needs a tester whose plant nobody else runs.
- Sigenergy's per-phase import-limit hold is field-tested; Sigenergy's **battery control** as a whole is still not marked field-tested, so outside beta tester mode it only logs what it would do.
- The days gone by in the Plan tab start with this update; earlier days show nothing.
- The SpotNav app does not yet show the new fields (the wear setting, the import-limit tap, the site's rows in Get started); an older app ignores them.

## How to get the beta

1. **Make a Home Assistant backup first** (Settings → System → Backups).
2. In HACS, open SpotNav, the menu (three dots), *Redownload*, switch on *Show beta versions* and choose `3.0.0b4`.
3. Restart Home Assistant, then hard-refresh the browser (or reset the app's frontend cache).

Already on an earlier 3.0 beta? *Update* is enough. Going back to 2.5.x: use **2.5.5 or later**, as described in the 3.0.0b1 notes.
