SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.5.8 keeps planning a charge to a target when the car's charge level cannot be read.

## Fixes

- **A car whose charge level cannot be read is still planned for.** When the car's level sensor went `unavailable`, for example because the login of the car's own integration had expired, SpotNav stopped planning a charge to a target. The card then said that something had to be fixed before charging could be planned and listed a missing setting called `live_soc`, although nothing in the settings could fix it. Now SpotNav plans from the estimate it keeps from the last reading and the energy the charger has delivered since. Without an estimate it plans from the last reading it has, while that reading is under a day old (the car may have been driven since an older one), and otherwise it plans a full charge to the target; the car ends that charge itself when it is full.
- **The status says it calmly.** A notice says that the car's level cannot be read and what the plan was made from, for example *The car's charge level can't be read; planning from the estimated 88 %*. It shows after 15 minutes without a reading, so a short cloud dropout says nothing, and not at all while the charger shows the car unplugged. When the car's integration asks for a new login, or failed to start, the notice names it at once (*Kia Connect needs you to sign in again*) and an administrator gets a button to **Settings → Devices & services**. The notice goes away by itself when the level reads again.
- **No raw setting names in the card.** The list of missing settings now shows words only. A battery size that is missing is called "battery size", and a name the card does not know is left out instead of being shown as it is.

Who is affected: everyone who charges to a target with a car whose level comes from a cloud integration. Other setups are not changed.

Nothing needs doing after the update.
