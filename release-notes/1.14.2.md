SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.14.2 tries a paused charge again while a home battery fills the fuse, and says so, and tells a new plan only when it is news.

## Load balancing

- **A paused charge is tried again.** When a home battery began its own grid charge in the same cheap hours as the car and load balancing paused the car, the charge could stay paused for hours, even while the plan's window was open: a stopped OCPP charger reports no current, and SpotNav waited for a reading that never came. Now SpotNav starts the car again at its minimum current, so the battery gets the chance to give way, and checks within half a minute that the fuse is no more than half an amp over. If the battery does not give way, it tries again after 10 minutes, then every quarter of an hour for as long as the charge is wanted. It never tries while the house itself, not a charging battery, uses the fuse.
- **The car starts at its minimum beside a charging battery.** When the home battery charges from the grid (or is just starting to), a start gives the car its minimum current instead of the whole plan's current, and yield stepping raises it as the battery gives way. With the battery idle a start is as before.
- **One reading, one step down.** Load balancing no longer lowers the car again and again on the same meter reading: it waits for the next reading to see what its first step did, and lowers again at once if the fuse is still over.

## Card and app

- **The status says why the car waits.** While load balancing holds the plan's charge back inside its window, the status reads, for example, *Paused – the house is using the whole fuse; trying again at 04:30*, or names the home battery when that is why. An app that does not know this line yet says that load balancing gives the car 0 A instead.

## Notifications

- **No new plans while the car is away.** With the car unplugged and on the road, its changing state of charge gave a new plan every hour, and each one reached the phones and woke the SpotNav app. Now SpotNav keeps the plan up to date without telling it while the charger says no car is plugged in, and after the plug-in tells the plan made for the car once. A charger that cannot tell whether a car is there tells as before.
- **One notification for a few quick changes.** A new plan is told half a minute after it was made, and only if no other plan followed, so a few changes in a row give one notification for the plan they ended in. Problems, a completed charge and the question which car is plugged in still go out at once.
- **No night-time notification when a window ends.** When the plan's first window ended and the rest of the same plan was installed, that was told as a new plan. What is left of the plan last told is no longer told; a real change to it still is.
