SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.14.2 tries a paused charge again while a home battery fills the fuse, and says so, tells a new plan only when it is news, and stops chasing a car that is already full.

## Load balancing

- **A paused charge is tried again.** When a home battery began its own grid charge in the same cheap hours as the car and load balancing paused the car, the charge could stay paused for hours, even while the plan's window was open: a stopped OCPP charger reports no current, and SpotNav waited for a reading that never came. Now SpotNav starts the car again at its minimum current, so the battery gets the chance to give way, and checks within half a minute that the fuse is no more than half an amp over. If the battery does not give way, it tries again after 10 minutes, then every quarter of an hour for as long as the charge is wanted. It never tries while the house itself, not a charging battery, uses the fuse.
- **The car starts at its minimum beside a charging battery.** When the home battery charges from the grid (or is just starting to), a start gives the car its minimum current instead of the whole plan's current, and yield stepping raises it as the battery gives way. With the battery idle a start is as before.
- **One reading, one step down.** Load balancing no longer lowers the car again and again on the same meter reading: it waits for the next reading to see what its first step did, and lowers again at once if the fuse is still over.

## Target charge level

- **The energy delivered counts again at a charger with two cars.** Between two readings of a car's cloud, SpotNav carries the level forward from the energy the charger delivered. With more than one car at a charger, every look at the card or the app threw that estimate away: the list of the cars read the other car's level too, and the car being charged was counted from its old reading again. A car whose cloud said 72 % all night was planned for from 72 % until the morning, although it had been given enough to be full. Now a car that is only shown never changes the estimate of the car the charger plans for, and the estimate never goes past the car's own charge limit.
- **A car that stops taking the charge is noticed.** When the car takes nothing for five minutes inside a planned period (an OCPP charger says the car is suspended, or the charger measures at most 1 A), the car has ended the charge. If its level, read or estimated, says it is full for the plan, the plan ends there: the charge is stopped, the rest of the plan is cleared, the card says what level it stopped at and a charge to the car's own limit is told as complete with the car full. If not, the period open then is left alone and the next one charges as planned. Either way SpotNav asks the car once for a fresh reading, and a reading below the target is planned again. Before, only a charge a person started was watched for this, and a target at the car's own limit, which is the car's to end, went on being planned every half hour until the departure.
- **Waiting for the car after it ended the charge.** A car that ended the charge with an old reading the delivered energy already covers, or with an estimate that says it is full, is no longer planned for again while the plan still has a period ahead: the status says SpotNav waits for the car's new level.
- **No error at the departure.** A plan made for the last quarter before the departure, waiting for the period before it to end, was applied only when it was itself over and shown as an execution error. It is now dropped quietly.

## Card and app

- **A stop at the target is told only while it is true, and with its time.** After the target stopped a charge, the status went on saying so, as *Stopped at 53 % (just now)*, until a new plan or a person's Start: three hours later beside the sun about to start, and even while the sun charged the car again after it had been driven. Now the line ends when a charge starts or the charger is seen charging, whoever started it, when the car is unplugged or another car is identified, when the car reports a level well below the target, and when the target is raised above the level it stopped at. It never follows a status that says the car is charging, and it says when the charge was stopped, for example *Stopped at 53 % at 07:31*.
- **The status says why the car waits.** While load balancing holds the plan's charge back inside its window, the status reads, for example, *Paused – the house is using the whole fuse; trying again at 04:30*, or names the home battery when that is why. An app that does not know this line yet says that load balancing gives the car 0 A instead.

## Notifications

- **No new plans while the car is away.** With the car unplugged and on the road, its changing state of charge gave a new plan every hour, and each one reached the phones and woke the SpotNav app. Now SpotNav keeps the plan up to date without telling it while the charger says no car is plugged in, and after the plug-in tells the plan made for the car once. A charger that cannot tell whether a car is there tells as before.
- **One notification for a few quick changes.** A new plan is told half a minute after it was made, and only if no other plan followed, so a few changes in a row give one notification for the plan they ended in. Problems, a completed charge and the question which car is plugged in still go out at once.
- **No night-time notification when a window ends.** When the plan's first window ended and the rest of the same plan was installed, that was told as a new plan. What is left of the plan last told is no longer told; a real change to it still is.
