SpotNav for Home Assistant 3.0.0b3 is the third beta of 3.0. **Learns your home. Plans your energy.**

This is still a beta, offered only to those who switch on beta versions in HACS (or the Marketplace). 2.5.x stays the normal release. Everything new that could write to a battery or a heat pump still starts in **shadow** or behind its own opt-in, as in b1 and b2.

## What is new

- **The Plan tab on the house card.** Everything SpotNav plans for the home on one rolling time axis, from the running hour to the end of the prices known (at most 36 hours): what the house takes from the grid each hour, split by load (the house, the heat pump and the water heater, each car, the home battery), the sun and the battery covering the house below zero, the net from the grid, and the ceiling that applies (the import target, the power level, the power tariff's ceiling, else the main fuse) as a labelled dashed line, with a price strip under it. Under the chart **Per load** (a lane and a line of why per load) and **In words** (the plan as a list); both fold away under their headings, folded until you open them, with a hint of what is inside, remembered per site. A load SpotNav only watches is drawn faded and says *would*. See [The house card](docs/house-card.md#plan).
- **When a car's charge does not fit under the ceiling.** Where an import target or a power level holds the hour and the car cannot be ready by its departure under it, the charging card, the Plan tab and the phones say so, with three choices for **this departure only**: **Take what fits (≈ 12 kWh)**, **Later departure (17:00)** (the earliest whole hour by which all of it fits; pressing it moves this one departure, never the daily departure, its weekdays or the calendar) and **Allow the peak (≈ 12 kW, ≈ 105 kr)**. The notice keeps the old wording that names the ceiling ("The car will not be ready by 07:00 under the 6.0 kW import target – 4 kWh short", or "under the month's peak of 7.5 kW (import target 6.0 kW)"), with the choices as buttons. It is told once per departure, never between 22:00 and 07:00 (one that arises at night is told at 07:00 if it still stands), and a car plugged in later in the day is told at the plug-in. It is taken off the phones once the charge fits again or a choice is made, on the phone, in the card or in the app. Unanswered, the import target holds. See [Holding the chargers under the import target](docs/site-and-load-balancing.md#holding-the-chargers-under-the-import-target).
- **One shed order under the ceiling.** When the hour is heading over the import target or power level, the loads give way in a fixed order: **home battery → water heater → car → heat**, and come back in the reverse order. The battery's help counts first, as before. A plain water heater with control on is then paused to the end of the hour, only in one of its cheap windows that still covers what it needs, after every legionella rule, never once 16 hours have passed since its last full heat, and only on a meter SpotNav trusts. The car is held as before, but now only for what is left. The heat is lowered one step (the controlled zones 1 °C) only once the hour is over even with the cars paused, within its own rules (at most 3 hours in a row and 6 a day, never in a cold room). There is no setting for the order in this beta. The main fuse keeps its own protection and is never part of it, and no new notice is sent. See [The shed order](docs/site-and-load-balancing.md#the-shed-order-under-the-ceiling).
- **Grid charging the home battery on a small main fuse (Sigenergy).** On a 16–20 A fuse the rule that reckons with Home Assistant stopping mid-charge left little or no grid charge. A Sigenergy plant with *Independent phase power control* on holds each phase at a third of its own grid import limit by itself, whatever Home Assistant does; this was **field-tested on site** on a SigenStor EC 12.0 TP on 2026-11-21. Where you say the limit acts, and SpotNav reads the phase control on, the output type L1/L2/L3/N, the grid sensor connected and the limit at most 3 × (fuse − safety margin) × voltage, the grid charge runs at the plan's power and the plant takes what the house leaves. Every reading is checked again at every start and every look. The battery settings say the one step still missing. An on-site test script for other Sigenergy plants is included (`tools/sigen_import_limit_test.py`). See [Grid charging on a small main fuse](docs/home-battery.md#grid-charging-on-a-small-main-fuse-sigenergy).
- **Accepting the risk if Home Assistant stops (other brands).** For a battery without such a guard of its own, an administrator can accept, in the battery settings, that a fuse may trip if Home Assistant stops (or loses the battery or the meter) during a grid charge of the home battery while the house draws a lot. The grid charge is then capped at what the fuse allows with an empty house, instead of the cautious cap. Everything that works while Home Assistant runs is unchanged: the per-phase check every 10 seconds, the write caps, read-back and hand-back. It is offered only where it makes a real difference, never for a battery SpotNav cannot lower within seconds (Tesla), and it is turned off again if the main fuse or the battery changes. See [Accepting the risk](docs/home-battery.md#accepting-the-risk-if-home-assistant-stops-other-brands).
- **The home battery's round trip is learnt.** Instead of a fixed 90 %, SpotNav learns your battery's round-trip efficiency from stretches that start and end at the same level with at least 20 kWh charged, read from the battery's own lifetime counters where the integration has them (Sigenergy, Huawei, SolarEdge, SolaX, Sofar, Solinteg, GoodWe ET, FoxESS, Victron with a battery monitor, Enphase), else from the measured power. The plan and the learnt size use it, and the house card's details say "Round-trip efficiency: 84 %, learnt" or "assumed 90 %". No setting. See [The battery plan](docs/battery-plan.md).
- **A hardware test that can pass on a real site.** While the test's battery part runs, SpotNav holds every charger on the site stopped; an OCPP charger is taken out of service through the `ocpp` integration's availability switch (never a charging profile) and put back afterwards, also after a restart. A test with a battery part does not start while a car charges, and a charge started during it has to be started again afterwards. The learned per-phase peaks now count load the plant sees on no phase, and the test's short charge step may go up to 98 %. See [Beta testing](docs/beta-testing.md).
- **Sigenergy DC charger.** The charging power you set starts at the first whole ampere above the charger's own lowest power (3 A, 2.1 kW at 400 V) and is named in kW, also where load balancing, the import target or the sun holds it. Its start is no longer held back while the home battery fills the fuse. A lower minimum once a charge runs (1.0 kW) is built but **off until a tester confirms** that the charger holds it: the checklist is in [Set up](docs/setup.md#sigenergy-dc-charger).
- **Power tariff: the car's step, in shadow.** Where the electricity price has a demand charge, SpotNav now also plans each car once more under the soft ceiling, with the cars' likely charges for the rest of the month counted from their own habits, and costs both plans. It only logs and shows this in the diagnostics: nothing is held back for it. A tariff part can also carry its grid company's peak rules as data (a quarter-hour period, a least billed kW, a price step); where a tariff counts quarter hours, the import target holds the running quarter hour. See [Prices](docs/prices.md#a-power-level-and-the-demand-charge-in-shadow-30-beta).
- **Vehicle identification at night.** A sure camera answer can now decide at night for a car that has a night reference picture, and an unknown colour no longer vetoes a sure answer. A sure camera decision is corrected only by a plug sensor that changed at this plug-in, or the car seen at another charger: a tracker's position alone never does. See [Vehicle identification](docs/vehicle-identification.md).
- **Smaller changes.** The heating card's strip is a rolling **24 hours** across midnight. Thermia Genesis lowers its comfort wheel by **one step** (3 °C of supply on the whole curve), also when it is cold. The charging card's chart opens on **From now** for anyone who has not chosen an axis; a saved choice is kept. The heat pump's pulse input is now called **Hot water boost**, kept apart from the **Water heater** switch (the settings refuse the same switch for both); SpotNav pulses it only when it reads off and afterwards turns off only what its own pulse turned on.

## Fixes

- **A plain water heater's meter was never read** on a real install in b2, so every water heater ran as if it had no meter (the 5-hour night window). It is read now, and a zone's meter likewise. The water heater's meter is trusted only once it has followed SpotNav's own switching (an off taking it from the element's draw to under 100 W, or an on raising it, within two minutes); until then the heater runs as unmetered, and a later off it does not follow takes the trust back.
- **Easee and a car that finished by itself:** SpotNav no longer sends a window's start again and again to a car that had finished its charge; one try per plan, again when the target or departure changes, the car's level drops or it is unplugged. A new plan no longer sends a stop to a charger that says no car is there.
- **The car's charge limit** is chosen by its entity, not by what it reads: an AC limit reading 0 (seen on a Kia EV6 at night) no longer falls back to the DC limit, and a limit that drops below the car's level during a charge is held, so a plan that stopped at its target is not taken for one the car ended.
- **SolaX:** the firmware is read from the inverter's software version, so battery control no longer waits forever on an unknown firmware (the same fallback for any brand whose firmware entity reads nothing), and the size from *Battery System Installed Capacity* on GEN4 to GEN6 as well as GEN3's *Battery Install Capacity*.
- **Fixes from beta reports:** the house forecast reads the outdoor sensor chosen in the heat settings first; the plan's energy, cost and distance in the status are capped at what fits in the car; the grid power's age is its freshest entity's (an export half at 0 W no longer makes the meter look old); hours lowered only because the house was already warm are left out of the heat review.
- **The debug info** now carries SpotNav's own INFO log lines even at Home Assistant's default log level, without adding them to Home Assistant's log; the day records carry SpotNav's version; a plug-in's car is recorded also when the car is decided after the unplug.
- **Quieter log:** load balancing's held decisions and similar per-look lines moved to debug. The hardware test's taking out of service and setting back run in the background and never hold up Home Assistant's start; a charger that cannot be set back gives one warning (and a Repairs issue) instead of one per retry.

## What to do after updating

- **Open the house card's Plan tab** to see the whole plan. *Per load* and *In words* start folded: tap the heading.
- **Import target or power level users:** the plan's *If the car cannot be ready in time* is **Ask** by default, which now sends the does-not-fit notice with its buttons to the phones chosen for *the charge will not be ready in time*. Check those phones in the charger's notification settings.
- **Sigenergy on a 16–20 A fuse:** follow the four steps in [Grid charging on a small main fuse](docs/home-battery.md#grid-charging-on-a-small-main-fuse-sigenergy): enable `Grid Import Limitation`, `Grid Sensor Status`, `Output Type` and `Independent phase power control` in the Sigenergy integration, turn the phase control on, lower the plant's grid import limit in the Sigenergy app to at most 3 × (fuse − safety margin) × voltage (13.1 kW on 20 A with a 1 A margin), and turn on **The import limit acts** in the battery settings. The battery section says which step is still missing.
- **Another brand on a small fuse:** read [Accepting the risk](docs/home-battery.md#accepting-the-risk-if-home-assistant-stops-other-brands) before ticking it.
- **SolaX:** enable *Battery System Installed Capacity* (GEN4 to GEN6) or *Battery Install Capacity* (GEN3) in the SolaX Modbus integration, both disabled by default, so the size is read rather than learnt.
- **A plain water heater:** check that its own thermostat is set to **at least 60 °C**. Its meter is trusted after the first switching SpotNav makes; until then it runs on the night window.
- **Sigenergy DC charger owners:** if you can, run the five-step check for the lower running minimum in [Set up](docs/setup.md#sigenergy-dc-charger) and send debug info.
- **Do not start a car charge during the hardware test**; stop a running one before you start a test with a battery part.

## Known limits

- The shed order is fixed (battery → water heater → car → heat); a setting may come later. A water heater without a trusted meter is never shed, as there is nothing to count it as giving. The heat is shed only where *Control the heat* is on; otherwise it says what it would do.
- The power tariff's soft ceiling, for the battery and the car, is **shadow only**: it holds nothing back, makes no "does not fit" and sheds nothing. Around the switch to summer time, the last days of March may still be counted one hour off.
- *Later departure* is offered only where a later whole hour fits within the prices held; otherwise the button opens the departure's editor (on the charging card) or is not shown (in the Plan tab).
- The does-not-fit notice comes from the import target's live hold, so it needs the car plugged in with a plan: a recurring departure with the car away gets no notice at about 13:00.
- Sigenergy's per-phase import-limit hold is field-tested; Sigenergy's **battery control** as a whole is still not marked field-tested, so outside beta tester mode it only logs what it would do.
- The Sigenergy AC charger and the DC charger's lower running minimum have not yet run on a real charger. The Thermia Genesis path has not yet run on a real pump.
- The plan's "with or without SpotNav" comparison in the Plan tab is not there yet.
- The SpotNav app shows the plan and the does-not-fit choices from its coming update; an older app ignores them.

## How to get the beta

1. **Make a Home Assistant backup first** (Settings → System → Backups).
2. In HACS, open SpotNav, the menu (three dots), *Redownload*, switch on *Show beta versions* and choose `3.0.0b3`.
3. Restart Home Assistant, then hard-refresh the browser (or restart the app).

Already on 3.0.0b1 or b2? *Update* is enough. Going back to 2.5.x: use **2.5.5 or later**, as described in the 3.0.0b1 notes.
