SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.3.0

## Fixes

- **Solar charging goes past 16 A.** The sun never asked a charger for more than 16 A, whatever the charger could do: a 32 A single-phase charger took at most 3.7 kW of sun and sent the rest to the grid, a 32 A three-phase one at most 11 kW. The sun now goes up to the charger's own maximum, the same top as the card's current slider (32 A when the charger states none), and never above the charging current set in Auto, as a planned charge. Load balancing still keeps every phase within the fuse, and a charger whose maximum changes, or that switches to one phase, is followed at once.
