SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.13.0 works out which car is plugged in when several cars share a charger, from the cars' own reports, a camera or a quick question on your phone, and gives the card's settings a new, compact look.

## Which car is plugged in?

When two or more cars can charge at a charger, SpotNav now finds out which one was plugged in and plans from that car's level, battery and target. With one car nothing changes and nothing is asked. How it works, step by step, is in [Which car is plugged in?](https://github.com/henrikekblad/spotnav-home-assistant/blob/main/docs/vehicle-identification.md).

- **From the cars themselves.** At a plug-in SpotNav reads each car's charging cable sensor and position. A car whose cable says plugged in is the one; a car that has just reported being away from home, or is already at another SpotNav charger, is not. When that settles it, SpotNav picks the car without asking.
- **Otherwise it asks.** When the cars cannot tell, SpotNav asks *Which car is plugged in?* on the phones chosen under **Notifications**, with one button per car, through the Home Assistant Companion app or the SpotNav app. The card shows the same question as a banner. The first answer wins, from any phone or the card, and the notification on every phone then quietly says which car was selected. No answer keeps the car that was already chosen.
- **A person's choice wins.** An answer, or a car chosen in the settings, always beats what the cars report. The card's car line says how the car was decided (by the car's charging cable, by position, by the camera, selected manually, or assumed), and tapping it (**⇄ Change car**) changes the car at any time while a car is plugged in.
- **The charge target follows the car.** Each car now has its own charge target, the same at every charger. When the car at a charger changes, its own target comes with it; the departure stays the charger's.
- **Settings.** In the card's settings, **Cars at this charger** lists the cars that can charge and be planned for there, and **Identification** is **Automatic** (the default), **Always ask** or **Off**. Each car's plug sensor and position can be chosen in the car's settings when SpotNav finds more than one. They are only read to tell the cars apart, never to start or stop a charge.

## Camera identification

A camera that sees the parking spot can tell the cars apart too, through a Home Assistant AI Task with a vision model.

- **Setting it up.** Under the charger in the card's settings, choose the **Camera**, **Crop parking spot** by dragging a selection around the spot (only what is inside is compared, so other cars and the street stay out), and choose the **AI task**. Then, with each car parked at the charger, take its **Reference picture**: a day picture, and in the dark a night picture for the camera's infrared. A car without a reference picture is never recognised by the camera.
- **Which model?** A local 7B vision model, such as `qwen2.5vl:7b` in Ollama, is recommended: it answers in about 20 seconds on an ordinary home server. Smaller models were seen to guess and still be sure.
- **When the camera decides.** The camera picks the car on its own only when the model is sure, it is daylight, every car has a reference picture and the car it names has a clearly different colour from the others. In every other case, such as two dark cars or at night, it only puts the likeliest car first in the question. A car's own report and your answer always outrank the camera.
- **Your pictures stay in Home Assistant.** Reference pictures are kept in Home Assistant's private storage and its backups, never in a public folder or a notification. With a local model they never leave your home.

## The card

- **"Identifying the car…" and "Waiting for an answer".** While SpotNav works out which car was plugged in, the status line leads with *Identifying the car…*, and while the question is open with *Waiting for an answer: which car is plugged in?*. Both go as soon as the car is decided.
- **Compact settings.** The card's settings are now short value rows under the headings Charger, Site and Car, as in the SpotNav app. Tap a value to change it in its own small editor; after saving, the settings stay where you were. Several cars appear as tabs of one car section.

## Diagnostics

- **An identification history.** The charger's diagnostics and the card's **Download debug info** now keep the last ten plug-ins: which cars could be the one, what each car reported, what the camera answered and how long it took, the question and its answer, and how the car was decided. Sending it with a report shows exactly why a car was picked. It names no place and holds no picture.

## Fixed

- **No "new plan" notification after a restart with the same plan.** SpotNav now remembers, per charger, the last plan it told you about or that followed a change of your own (its charging periods, the energy planned and the car it is for), also across restarts and updates. A plan that comes out the same after Home Assistant starts is not notified, to your phone or to the app; any real change still is.
- **Solar follows the sun without load balancing.** On a site without active load balancing (including a site that measures current per phase, and a charger whose phase is not set) the charging current now follows the solar surplus as it should. Before, the current asked for was only recorded and the car kept charging at the current it started with. The current never goes above the charging current you set.
- **No early stop when the charger's reading lags.** A solar start that counted on the house battery's charge is no longer stopped in its first seconds just because the charger had not yet reported the car's draw.
- **Solar leaves an empty charger alone.** A charger with no car plugged in is no longer started by solar, and no longer holds on to the sun's surplus while another charger could use it. The current also no longer moves up and down by one amp on every reading when the surplus hovers at a whole amp.

## For SpotNav app users

The SpotNav app's own *Which car is plugged in?* notification, with a button per car that answers even with the app closed, needs SpotNav app 1.1. With an older app, answer through the Home Assistant Companion app or the card.

After updating, reload the browser. In the Home Assistant Companion app, force-stop the app (Android settings → Apps → Home Assistant) if the card says it cannot read the integration's answer.
