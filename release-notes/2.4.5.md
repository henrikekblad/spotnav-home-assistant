SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.4.5 keeps quiet about the plan while you have stopped a charge, and sees the car leave a charger that cannot tell.

## Fixes

- **A charge you stop by hand no longer sends notices about the plan.** While your Stop holds, no notice is sent that a new plan is made, that the charge won't be ready in time, or that a charge did not start as planned. The card still shows the plan under the paused line. The notices come back once you press Start or Resume, or unplug the car.
- **A charger that cannot tell whether a car is plugged in while it is stopped (Charge Amps through its cloud integration) now learns of an unplug from the car's own plug sensor.** Your Stop lasts until the car is unplugged, but a Charge Amps connector that is turned off says `Unavailable` with or without a car, so the unplug was never seen and the Stop could hold for days. Now the car's plug sensor (the one shown for the car in SpotNav's settings) tells the unplug and the next plug-in at home (a car plugged in elsewhere, by its location, does not count), as long as it reported after the Stop; the charger's own reading always comes first. Without such a sensor, the card says to press Start or Resume.
- **A Start pressed after the car left unseen no longer leaves SpotNav paused for a car that is gone.** At a charger that cannot tell whether a car is plugged in while it is stopped, pressing Start turns it on, and it then says no car. The Start now ends with that unplug, like any Start whose car is unplugged: Auto takes over again, and nothing charges until a car is plugged in.

## Improved

- **Peblar is confirmed on real hardware.** A user has charged with a Peblar through SpotNav, and its switch to one phase for solar works. The list of supported hardware says so.
- **Run on solar surplus can also switch on while electricity is cheap.** A new optional input, Also on while cheap, takes the price device's Cheap now sensor: the load is then on at a surplus or while it is cheap, and off only when both are gone, with the same minimum times, window and allowed entity. Left empty, nothing changes. An edited copy of the blueprint is kept as it is.
