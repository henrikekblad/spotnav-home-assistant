SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.4.5 keeps quiet about the plan while you have stopped a charge, and sees the car leave a charger that cannot tell.

## Fixes

- **A charge you stop by hand no longer sends notices about the plan.** While your Stop holds, no notice is sent that a new plan is made, that the charge won't be ready in time, or that a charge did not start as planned. The card still shows the plan under the paused line. The notices come back once you press Start or Resume, or unplug the car.
- **A charger that cannot tell whether a car is plugged in while it is stopped (Charge Amps through its cloud integration) now learns of an unplug from the car's own plug sensor.** Your Stop lasts until the car is unplugged, but a Charge Amps connector that is turned off says `Unavailable` with or without a car, so the unplug was never seen and the Stop could hold for days. Now the car's plug sensor (the one shown for the car in SpotNav's settings) tells the unplug and the next plug-in, as long as it reported after the Stop; the charger's own reading always comes first. Without such a sensor, the card says to press Start or Resume.
