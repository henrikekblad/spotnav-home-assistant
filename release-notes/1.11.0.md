SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.11.0 lets a Start or Stop you press take over from the automation until the car is unplugged, charges as much as it can when the departure comes too soon, and makes charger control more robust.

## New

- **Your Start or Stop wins.** Pressing Start or Stop (card, app, button) pauses automatic charging – schedule and strategy – until the car is unplugged. The status says so: "Stopped manually – until the car is unplugged." or "Charging manually – until the car is full or unplugged." A manual Start ends by itself when the car is full, and a Stop pressed with no car plugged in holds for the next plug-in. Resume automatic charging ends it at once.
- **A departure that comes too soon** no longer stops planning. SpotNav charges in every quarter-hour left and says how far it gets: "Will not be ready by the departure: about 44 % (7.4 kWh) by 12:00.", and the "not ready by the departure" notification is sent.
- **A charger that keeps charging after it was stopped** is noticed: after three stops in ten minutes SpotNav sends no more, says so and notifies you.
- A Start with no car plugged in is refused with a clear message.

## Charger control

- A period's end stops only the plan's own charge; one you started or the sun started goes on.
- A stop the charger did not take is retried instead of being counted as done, and never makes setup or a new plan fail.
- Easee: a short "unavailable" in the charger's status is no longer taken as a new plug-in, and a current change can no longer lift a pause that was just sent.
- Several chargers starting at once share the main fuse's margin, and a lower current for an overload goes out at once, without waiting for another charger.
- More of the charging state survives a Home Assistant restart.

## Settings

- A site that reads each phase's current from one sensor's attributes (such as the Easee Equalizer) shows that source, and its settings can be saved again.
- The plan's blocking messages say exactly why no plan could be made.
- An energy meter that cannot be read for a moment (such as right after a restart) no longer says so: the plan is made again as soon as it reads, and only a meter unread for half an hour is reported.
- The card no longer shows "Configuration error" now and then after a page reload.

After updating, reload the browser. In the Home Assistant Companion app, force-stop the app (Android settings → Apps → Home Assistant) if the card says it cannot read the integration's answer.
