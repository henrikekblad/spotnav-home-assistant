SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.2.0 counts every charger's energy with the right meter and says what a plan really buys.

## Fixes

- **A plan for a fixed amount now says the amount you asked for, and its cost.** A need SpotNav counts with the charger's energy meter ends the charge as soon as the meter shows it delivered, but the card stated the energy and cost of whole quarter-hours (30.5 kWh asked, 33.3 kWh and its cost shown). The plan now states 30.5 kWh, counts the last quarter-hour's cost only for the part the need takes, and the card's plan and its *Charging now; scheduled until* time end where the need is expected to be met. A charger without an energy meter is unchanged: nothing stops it early, so its whole quarter-hours are charged and shown.
- **Alfen (Modbus) counts all three phases.** SpotNav took the energy of phase L1 alone for the charger's meter, so a three-phase charge counted a third of what it delivered and a fixed amount ran on far past it, until the car was full or the departure came. SpotNav now reads *Real energy delivered sum*.
- **go-e Charger (legacy) and Fronius Wattpilot count with their lifetime meter.** SpotNav could take the charger's per-session counter for its meter (go-e's *Current Session charged*, Wattpilot's *Connection Charged*); it now takes the lifetime one (*Total Charged*, *Totally Charged*). Heidelberg Energy Control's per-session counter is now known as well, so it is never taken for the lifetime *Total energy*.
- **myenergi zappi, ABB Terra AC, Ohme, ChargePoint and Hypervolt count their energy from the charger's own power.** These integrations have no lifetime energy meter, and SpotNav took a per-session or daily counter (the zappi's *Energy used today*) or none. It now counts the energy from the charger's power sensor itself, as it does for a charger behind a smart plug, also when the integration's cloud reports only every few minutes; a gap in the readings is never filled with a guess. *Download diagnostics* says what each charger's energy is counted with.
- **A meter SpotNav chose wrongly is replaced when you update.** A charger added before 2.2.0 with one of those counters gets the right meter, or its power, at the first start, and the log says so. A separate energy meter of another integration is always kept. Before 2.2.0 SpotNav did not remember whether a meter was its suggestion or your choice: if you picked one of those counters on purpose, choose it again under *Energy* in the card, and from then on it is kept.
