SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.15.2 lets more chargers say that no car is plugged in, so a plan no longer starts and no notice goes out at an empty charger.

## Charger

- **No car is read from the charger's status.** Since 1.14.2 a window that opens with no car plugged in starts nothing, and the phones are not told that the charge will not be ready, did not start or has finished. That needed the charger to say no car is there, and only Easee, Wallbox, Zaptec, OpenEVSE and OCPP chargers could: the card already showed *disconnected* for many more, but the part that starts the charge and sends the notices took them as unable to tell. Now both read the charger's status the same way, so Charge Amps, go-e, Wattpilot, Peblar, NRGkick, Lektrico, Alfen, Heidelberg, GARO, DEFA, ABB Terra AC, Ohme and Monta chargers also say when no car is plugged in. Plugging in and unplugging are then seen too, so the car is identified at the plug-in, and a car plugged in inside a planned period starts charging. Only a status that clearly means no car counts; a status that can show while a car waits to be authorised still reads as unknown.
- **Charge Amps.** A Charge Amps reports *Unavailable* while its connector is switched off, so the first period of a night still switches it on; seconds later it says *Available*, and from then on nothing more is started or told while no car is plugged in.
