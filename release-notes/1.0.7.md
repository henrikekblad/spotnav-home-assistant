SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.0.7 makes Easee chargers more reliable and stops SpotNav from reporting commands that never reached the charger.

## Fixes

- **Easee: confirmed current limits.** SpotNav read the charger's dynamic limit from a place Easee's integration does not report it, so its writes were never confirmed and a restored limit could be reported as failed. It now reads Easee's own "Dynamic charger limit" sensor. That sensor is disabled by default; setup offers to enable it, and without it writes are treated as sent rather than failed.
- **Easee: starts at 7 A or more.** Easee's firmware can delay a start at 6 A by several minutes, so a start or a re-sent limit is never below 7 A.
- **Easee: no resume behind your back after a restart.** A charger SpotNav had paused before Home Assistant restarted stays paused until SpotNav resumes it.
- **Commands to unavailable entities.** Home Assistant silently skips a command to an unavailable switch, select or button. SpotNav now notices and reports that the start or stop was not carried out, instead of waiting for an acknowledgement that cannot come.
- **Wallbox: "waiting for car demand".** SpotNav now recognises Wallbox's status when the car is not asking for current.
- **OCPP: the session current limit** is written once the charging session exists, for chargers that only offer the limit during a session.

## Improved

- **The charger's own waiting states are explained.** When an Easee charger waits for its own schedule, smart charging or load balancer, the card says so instead of reporting an unanswered start.
