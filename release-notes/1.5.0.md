SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.5.0 adds a charge history with costs, learns the phases a car charges on, copes better with a home battery that shares the main fuse, and makes Easee chargers work with solar.

## New

- **Charge history.** Every charge is recorded with its energy, cost, saving against the day's average price and solar share. The card's History dialog shows one month at a time: a summary, a bar for every day coloured by its average price, the month's charges and Export CSV. Costs are worked out with the taxes and fees as they are set now, so changing them updates the history too.
- **Earlier charges imported.** Charges from before SpotNav, up to two years back, are imported once from Home Assistant's hourly energy statistics and priced from the day prices. They are marked "imported (hourly)".
- **Phases learned from charges.** A charge plans on the smaller of the charger's wiring and the car's onboard charger. Each vehicle has an onboard charger setting (1- or 3-phase), and when a car keeps charging on one phase the card suggests setting it. A charger in a site shows its wiring from the site.
- **Home battery on the same fuse.** When the battery gives way as the car takes more, SpotNav credits it and climbs faster, steps back one step on a short spike instead of starting over, and no longer steps the car down when the battery takes every amp it frees. The card says when the battery or the house holds the car below the plan.
- **Weekdays for the daily departure**, so a weekday-only departure plans to the next chosen day.
- **Charger priority on a site**: first, normal or last.
- **Event entities** per charger: charge started and finished, plugged in and unplugged, plan installed and plan at risk, for automations.
- **Download debug info** in the card settings (Support): versions, diagnostics, the recent SpotNav log and the regulator's decisions in one file, with secrets removed.
- **Safety margin in the card's Site dialog**, and a warning when a battery's own grid import limit differs from the fuse limit.
- On equal prices the plan charges as late as possible.

## Fixes

- **Easee with solar and load balancing**: the charger's current per phase is read from its Current sensor (enable it in the Easee integration; it is off by default), and the current limit is checked live.
- **Solar never charges from the grid on a fault**: when the charger's own measured current is missing, solar holds instead of cycling the charger, and stops once the grid shows sustained import.
- A waiting new plan says when it takes over.

After updating, reload the browser (or clear the app's frontend cache) so the new card loads.
