SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.15.0 asks the car before it stops at the target on an estimate, waits for the car's level after the car ended a charge, lets a wall screen start and pause a charge, and gives the chart a time axis that runs from now.

## Target charge level

- **An estimate at the target asks the car first.** When only the estimate from delivered energy said the target was reached, the charge was stopped on the estimate once it was a margin above the target. Now SpotNav keeps charging and asks the car's integration once to read the car again (the same re-read as the app's refresh, never a wake-up). A reading at or above the target stops the charge; a reading below it lets the charge go on, and the car is asked again only once the estimate has moved another percentage point. When no reading comes within ten minutes, or the car has nothing to re-read, the estimate alone stops the charge once it is 3 percentage points above the target.
- **The estimate never counts energy from before its reading.** Many chargers book energy late (an Easee's lifetime register can post an hour's charge at once), so energy charged before the car's reading could be counted again on top of it, and the estimate ran ahead of the car. A reading is now carried forward only from the first value the charger's register posts after it. The estimate can fall a little short of the car, never run ahead of it.
- **Waiting for the car's level after the car ended the charge.** When the car stopped taking the charge short of the target and its level then was only an estimate or a reading from before the end, the next period started the car again: on 14 October a car full at 100 % read 86 % by the estimate against a target of 91 %, and a car that drew nothing was shown as charging. Now the plan waits for the car's level: no period starts the car again, the status says *The car ended the charge at ~86 % (estimated); waiting for the car's level*, and SpotNav asks the car once for a reading. A reading below the target charges the car as planned, one at the target ends the plan. Unplugging, raising the target or a person's Start ends the wait too.
- **One stop, one completion.** A plan made right after a target stop that the same stop still satisfies ends on that stop, so a charge is told complete once.
- **A restart keeps the car's reading.** When Home Assistant restarts, the car's integration writes its last value again, stamped with the start, and SpotNav took it as a fresh reading: the energy charged since the real reading was forgotten and the level looked newer than it was. Now that value keeps the last reading's time and what it was measured from, and the energy before and after the restart both count. A value that moved, or a newer report from the car, is still a new reading.
- **A reading's age comes from the car.** Many car integrations have a sensor with the time the car itself last reported (Kia and Hyundai's *Last updated at*, Renault's *Last battery activity*, Polestar's battery data time, VW's *Last connected*, Ford's *Last refresh*, or a generic *Last updated*). When the car's device has exactly one such sensor, SpotNav takes a reading's age from it, so an hours-old value is no longer taken as fresh because Home Assistant wrote it just now. Without one, the age is taken as before.
- **A register that starts counting again keeps its count.** A per-session register that starts at zero at a plug-in or a restart, or a meter that reads 0 while its charger reboots, no longer makes the estimate lose what was charged before.

## Notifications

- **A completed charge says how the level is known.** On the car's reading the notice gives the target and the car's level: *Charging complete: the target 80 % was reached (the car shows 81 %).* On the estimate it never says the target was reached: *Charging stopped at 83 % (estimated), the target is 80 %.* A car that ended the charge itself just short of the target is told as *The car stopped charging at 79 %, just below the target 80 %.*
- **A car that ended the charge on an estimate is told once.** The end is told then, on the estimate, while the plan waits for the car's level; the period's end that follows is not told again. When the car's own level then says it is full, the end is told once more with that level.

## Card and app

- **A time axis from now.** Settings has a new **Display** section with **Chart time axis**: **Day** (00–24, tomorrow drawn over today, as before and the default) or **From now**. From now runs in real time from the current hour to the end of the last published day: nothing that has ended is drawn, tomorrow sits after today, a charging period across midnight is one band, and midnight is a dashed line named by its weekday and date. The highest and lowest price then cover everything shown. The choice belongs to the device: it is kept in the browser for every SpotNav card in it, and anyone can change it. The SpotNav app 1.3.0 has the same choice, and its widget follows it.
- **Wall screens can start and pause.** Start, Stop, Pause and Resume now work for every Home Assistant user, not only administrators, so a wall screen signed in as an ordinary user can start a charge or pause the schedule. The strategy, the plan and Settings stay an administrator's.
- **An administrator may let others change the strategy and plan.** A new charger setting, **Let users who are not administrators change strategy and plan** (Settings → Charger, off by default), lets such users choose the strategy and change the plan: the energy or target and its car, the finish-by time, date and weekdays, and the current. Everything else stays an administrator's, and Home Assistant refuses it from anyone else even when the card is bypassed.
- **Settings shows a non-administrator only what they may change.** The gear icon is shown to them only when Settings holds something they may change (today **Display**), and then only those sections. The price area is shown as a plain value.
- **The chart in a scaled view.** In a view that scales the card, such as a wall screen's fullscreen dashboard, the chart was squeezed sideways after it redrew. It now measures its width the way the page lays it out.
- **A smaller card.** The card carried all nine languages and now carries English only, about half its former size; another language is loaded from Home Assistant when the card starts, so for a moment the card shows an empty loading line. Changing the language in your profile is followed once the new words are in.

## Car identification

- **The camera's colour check looks at the car.** Two cars were compared by the average colour of each reference picture, and the ground around a car could make a dark blue car look like the empty driveway, or two cars of different colours look alike. Now the pictures are compared where they differ, which is where the cars are, with the light evened out on the ground they share. A car parked a little further on, or in other light, stays near itself. Reference pictures taken with an earlier release keep working: their colour is taken anew from the stored picture when SpotNav starts.

## Load balancing

- **A pause is logged once.** When load balancing paused a charge, the log could say so many times, as a warning, before the charger was seen off. It is now said once, as information. A safety stop stays a warning.
