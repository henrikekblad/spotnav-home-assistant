SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.8.0 adds Great Britain on Octopus Agile, a sensor that tells a home-battery planner when the car charges from the grid, and a few clearer dialogs.

## New

- **Great Britain (Octopus Agile).** The 14 Agile regions are available as price areas, grouped under Great Britain. Agile prices already include VAT and network costs, so those settings show "Included in the price" and nothing is added. Costs are in pounds and distances in miles. "Find my region" in the area dialog looks up your region from your postcode; Home Assistant asks Octopus Energy directly, and the postcode is not stored. SpotNav must not be used on Intelligent Octopus Go, where Octopus controls the charge itself.
- **"Charging from the grid" sensors.** Each charger, and each site, has a binary sensor that is on while the car charges from the grid (a plan window, the grid part of Hybrid, or Charge now) and off during solar charging. Point Predbat's `car_charging_now` at the site's sensor, or use it in your own automation to hold a home battery. SpotNav itself never writes to a battery: see the new Home batteries guide in the docs.
- **The price source** is named in the area dialog, under the area choice.
- **Newer price data.** SpotNav reads the price server's new format (with a fallback to the old one), which allows other market days and half-hour prices. Portugal is shown in Lisbon time.

## Improvements

- **"About this card"** moved into Settings → Support.
- **Site notes** (slow updates, estimated current, another load balancer…) are gathered under "To check" at the bottom of the Site dialog; a blocking measurement problem stays at the top.
- A site created before any charger says how to continue.
- An Easee charger's setup reminds you to turn off smart charging in Tibber or another app.
- The site sensors no longer slow Home Assistant down when they update.

After updating, reload the browser (or clear the app's frontend cache) so the new card loads.
