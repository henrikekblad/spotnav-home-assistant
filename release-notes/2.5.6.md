SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.5.6 fixes a car being charged to 100 % when its charge limit briefly read wrong.

## Fixes

- **A car's charge limit that briefly reads wrong no longer charges it past its target.** Some cars report two charge limits, one for AC and one for DC charging, for example Kia and Hyundai through the Kia Uvo / Hyundai Bluelink integration (kia_uvo). When the AC limit read an invalid value mid-charge (the car's cloud sent 0), SpotNav took the DC limit, often 80 %, as the car's limit instead. With a target above that, the plan treated the charge as one the car itself would stop, sent no stop at the target, and the charge could run on to 100 % at the end of the charging window. SpotNav now always uses the AC limit when the car has one, treats an invalid reading as unknown and keeps the last good limit, and during a charge ignores a limit that suddenly drops below the car's level. A limit you lower yourself is still followed; one set below the car's current level during a charge takes effect when that charge ends.

Who is affected: cars with a separate AC and DC charge limit, such as Kia and Hyundai through kia_uvo. Other cars are not changed.

Nothing needs doing after the update.
