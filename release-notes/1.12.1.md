SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.12.1 finds a charger's energy register after setup too.

## Charger control

- **The energy register is looked for again after setup.** A charger that was offline when it was added (an OCPP charger after a reinstall, say) often had no energy register yet, and SpotNav never looked again. Without it the car's level could not be carried forward from a stale cloud reading during a charge, so a reached target could be planned again. SpotNav now looks at every start and as soon as the charger's integration registers or first reports its register, and also accepts a register its integration has not yet given a class. A register that appears during a charge only counts from its first reading.
- **"None" for the energy meter is kept.** Choosing **None** under **Energy** in the card, or clearing a register in the integration's options, is remembered: SpotNav then reads no register and looks for none. While None is chosen, the charger's settings list a register it finds under **To check**, so you can choose it.

After updating, reload the browser. In the Home Assistant Companion app, force-stop the app (Android settings → Apps → Home Assistant) if the card says it cannot read the integration's answer.
