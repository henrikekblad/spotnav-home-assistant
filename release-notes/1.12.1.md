SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.12.1 adds a progress bar to the card, finds a charger's energy register after setup too, and no longer plans a reached target again on the car's stale level.

## Charger control

- **The energy register is looked for again after setup.** A charger that was offline when it was added (an OCPP charger after a reinstall, say) often had no energy register yet, and SpotNav never looked again. Without it the car's level could not be carried forward from a stale cloud reading during a charge, so a reached target could be planned again. SpotNav now looks at every start and as soon as the charger's integration registers or first reports its register, and also accepts a register its integration has not yet given a class. A register that appears during a charge only counts from its first reading.
- **"None" for the energy meter is kept.** Choosing **None** under **Energy** in the card, or clearing a register in the integration's options, is remembered: SpotNav then reads no register and looks for none. While None is chosen, the charger's settings list a register it finds under **To check**, so you can choose it.
- **The car's level is carried forward after a restart.** A level read while the energy register had no value yet (right after Home Assistant started) never moved with the energy delivered afterwards, so a target already reached could be planned again. The register's first value is now the starting point, a level the car's integration only sets again after a reload is the earlier reading while the charger saw the car plugged in all along, and a register that changes starts counting again instead of being compared with the old one. When in doubt (after a restart or an unplug) the level is taken as read: the car may be charged again, never left short.
- **A charged target waits for the car's new level.** Where the level cannot be carried forward, a target whose charge delivered, as measured, what the car's last reading needed is not planned again on that reading, on a charger that reports the car connected (a car found connected after a restart, or after the charger was offline, counts from then): the status says *Waiting for the car to report its new level after the charge*, and the car's integration is asked once to read it again. A newer reading, an unplug or a near departure plans again.

## The card

- **A progress bar while the car charges.** Under the status line a slim bar shows how far the charge has come and about when it is done (*62 % done · done about 14:35*, *93 % of target*), its stripes drifting slowly while current flows and standing still while the charge is paused. A target counts the car's level against the target while the schedule drives the charge; a Start, or a charge beside a paused schedule, counts against the car's own limit. Solar and hybrid give no end time, since the sun decides it, unless you pressed Start. With no level known the bar only says *Charging · 11 kW*. Where the charger measures its current or power, that is used for the end time. The charger's own *Charging* line steps aside while the bar shows. Home Assistant works the numbers out once, so the app shows the same bar.
- **"Schedule paused until …".** A paused schedule says so, so *Schedule paused until 22:00 · Charging now* no longer reads as if the charge were paused.
- **A charger with notifications on but no recipients says so.** Under **Notifications** in a charger's settings, a note says *No recipients chosen – this charger sends no notifications.* when events are switched on and no phone is chosen, since each charger keeps its own choice of phones.
- **Start and Stop show sooner.** After a Start or Stop the card reads the charger every five seconds until the charge follows (at most two minutes), then every 30 seconds as before.
- **"Starting…" and "Stopping…" while the charger answers.** Once Start or Stop is pressed, and while Home Assistant waits for the charger to report it, the Charging button says *Starting…* or *Stopping…* and the Schedule button keeps its caption, both greyed until the charger answers, as in the app. Before, the Schedule button disappeared until the charger answered a Start.
- **"Stopping…" holds until the charger has stopped.** Home Assistant now treats a Stop as it treats a Start: until the charger reports it has stopped, at most 30 seconds, it offers neither Start nor Resume, so the card and the app keep saying *Stopping…* instead of offering Start beside a charger that still charges. The Stop itself goes out at once as before, and if the charger still charges after 30 seconds, Stop is offered again.

## Under the hood

- The dashboard carries the charge's progress as a new `progress` block, and `live.measured_current_a` now states the charger's measured current where it has one (see `docs/api.md`).
- The debug info now keeps, per charger and across restarts, how often the new ownership model has seen each kind of event and how often it agreed with today's logic, so it is clear when it has seen enough to take over.

After updating, reload the browser. In the Home Assistant Companion app, force-stop the app (Android settings → Apps → Home Assistant) if the card says it cannot read the integration's answer.
