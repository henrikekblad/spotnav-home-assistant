SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.0.1 fixes two problems found when setting up a grid meter.

## Fixes

- **Choosing the grid meter manually no longer hangs.** SpotNav used to search the recorded history of every ampere sensor, which could take very long when a meter reports every second. The site's meter is now read live only, and the history search for a charger's own current is limited to the last two days and stops after ten seconds.
- **More P1 readers are detected.** ESPHome P1 readers are now recognised by their per-phase sensors instead of a specific device model. When both a P1 reader and an Easee Equalizer are found, the P1 reader is listed first.
