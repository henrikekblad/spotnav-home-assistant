SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.9.0 plans again when the car is plugged in or out, sends notifications to your phone, lets solar take care of a home battery's own decisions, stops charging when a car is full, and makes field problems much easier to see.

## New

- **Plug in, plug out.** SpotNav plans again when the car is plugged in, unplugged or the departure passes. Plugging in during a planned window starts the charge at once; a manual kWh plan stops when its energy is delivered, and what was delivered is remembered, also across an unplug or a Home Assistant restart. A Stop you press is kept for that window.
- **Notifications** through the Home Assistant Companion app: choose phones and events per charger under Settings → Notifications. On by default when you pick a phone: the planned charge stopped or did not start, the charge will not be ready by the departure, and charging is complete.
- **Charger priority** (First, Normal, Last) now also decides who gets solar surplus on a site with several chargers, and is shown in the settings overview.
- **A full battery is respected.** A manual kWh plan is capped at the room left in the car's battery when its charge level is known. Charging to the car's own limit (100 % or its charge limit) lets the car end the charge itself, and the charge may run up to an hour past the last window while the car tapers off.
- **Better debug info** (Settings → Support): the solar decisions, a minute-by-minute last hour of the site, the measurement sensors' raw values, and the card's own version, so an outdated card in a browser or the Companion app is spotted at once.

## Solar and hybrid

- **Solar starts at the minimum current** and checks for two minutes; a start that relied on a charging home battery stops at once if the battery turns to feed the car, and that credit then waits before it is used again.
- **A charge the charger started by itself** outside a plan window is taken over by solar: it follows the real surplus or stops.
- **A Stop you press sticks** until the car is plugged in again, you press Start, or a plan window begins — also across a restart.
- **When the car ends the charge itself**, solar says the car is full or when it tries again, instead of claiming it still charges.
- Solar keeps working on the total grid power when a phase measurement drops out (for example an inverter in standby), and never raises the current on a phase it cannot read.
- Hybrid no longer fails when the sun is expected to cover the whole need, and shows a wait for tomorrow's prices next to its headline.

## Meters

- A meter that reports negative current (export) while "Grid current is signed" is off is pointed out; detecting Tibber Pulse or the Easee Equalizer turns it on.
- A meter that updates too seldom for load balancing (such as the Easee Equalizer) is pointed out under "To check"; solar still uses it.

After updating, reload the browser. In the Home Assistant Companion app, force-stop the app (Android settings → Apps → Home Assistant) if the card says it cannot read the integration's answer.
