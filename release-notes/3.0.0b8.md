SpotNav for Home Assistant 3.0.0b8 is the eighth beta of 3.0. **Learns your home. Plans your energy.**

This is still a beta, offered only to those who switch on beta versions in HACS (or the Marketplace). 2.5.x stays the normal release. Everything new that could write to a battery, an inverter or a car still starts by only watching, or behind its own opt-in, as in the earlier betas.

## What is new

- **The year with SpotNav.** From 10 December to 31 January the house and charger cards offer a short story of your year: what SpotNav saved, how much of the car's charge went into the cheapest hours, the sun, the battery and the most expensive hour of the year, with a picture you can share. It works for recent installs too: what SpotNav did not see itself is read from Home Assistant's own statistics where it can be, marked ≈ where it is an estimate, and a page says "since …" where the history starts later. Once opened or closed the banner never comes back; the story stays under Settings → Support until 31 January. An administrator can show it at any time with the action `spotnav.show_year_review`. See [The year with SpotNav](docs/year-review.md).
- **A much faster battery plan.** The planner works out its limits and constants once per plan instead of millions of times, and uses numpy (part of every Home Assistant install) for its main search, with the same results in plain Python where numpy is missing. One hourly plan went from about 230 ms to about 5 ms (about 40×), a plan with a demand charge from about 330–390 ms to 13–15 ms (about 25×), measured on one desktop core; on a Raspberry Pi 4 that is from about 2–3 s to well under 0.1 s. Every plan is the same as before, checked byte for byte over three seasons of recorded data and about 83 000 demand-charge plans.
- **A new Now tab.** The house sits in the middle with its heat, pool and other use around it; sun, grid, battery and car at the corners. The lines grow with the power, the figures always add up (grid + sun = house + car + battery), anything estimated is marked ≈, and the drawing fills the card's width. Sun, heat and battery are read at the moment you look, from the plant's or each inverter's own power sensor. The card's graphs use the same colours for the same energy everywhere.
- **Single-phase sites** (Great Britain and others): a site whose meter measures L1 only is treated as single-phase for the fuse, load balancing, the battery's grid charge and sale, solar and the plans. A three-phase site never becomes single-phase by itself; a single-phase site whose charger draws on several phases stops regulating with a Repairs message.
- **Fixed Octopus tariffs (Flux, Go, Cosy, fixed):** until tomorrow's rates arrive in the afternoon, the battery plan plans tomorrow on today's pattern, so in the morning it already sees tonight's cheap window. This provisional day is used by the plan only: never stored, never in the day's record, replaced as soon as the real rates come. See [Prices](docs/prices.md).
- **Energy left in the battery at the plan's end** is valued at most at what a refill costs. This stops holding the battery full all day on tariffs with a fixed cheap night window; on Nordic prices it is about level, a little better in winter.
- **Victron through hass-victron:** the grid meter is found (the Multi's AC input only where there is no grid meter) and the battery's floor is read from its minimum SoC. A battery's rating is now the lowest of every limit SpotNav knows (BMS, inverter, plant), and the round trip is learned from the battery's power where its energy counters stand still.
- **Cars through the Tibber integration** (e.g. VW ID.7) are found as vehicles, although Tibber gives the range in metres.

## Fixes

- **The Energy graph keeps the house's hours** when a charger with no car keeps dropping offline: such a charger counts as 0 kW for the house. Hours a meter still missed are filled from the Energy dashboard's statistics about a day later.
- An offline charger whose last reading was an unplug gets no start or stop, and the "charger not ready" warning says "offline", logged once per outage.
- The Now tab takes less height, with its side nodes closer together.

## What to do after updating

- **After the update, reload the card:** hard-refresh the browser (Ctrl+Shift+R). In Home Assistant's companion app, reset its frontend cache (Android: Settings → Companion app → Troubleshooting → Reset frontend cache; iPhone and iPad: Settings → Companion app → Debugging → Reset frontend cache), then close the app fully and open it again. See [Troubleshooting](docs/troubleshooting.md).
- **Tibber car owners** who switched the range sensor to km by hand can leave it so; both work.

## Known limits

- Of the battery recipes, only Sigenergy has run under control on a real plant; Solis has passed the hold and hand-back on a real plant. Every other brand waits for its first tester.
- The year review's money pages need prices for the days they cover: with Nord Pool as the price source, Home Assistant holds about 55 days back, so those pages start later on such sites.
- Single-phase sites and the provisional Octopus day have run side by side on one real site, not yet under control.

## Before you downgrade

Going back to 3.0.0b7 works: what 3.0.0b8 stores is additive (the year review's own store, the filled house hours). On 3.0.0b7 the year review and its banner are gone and filled house hours are ignored.

## How to get the beta

1. **Make a Home Assistant backup first** (Settings → System → Backups).
2. In HACS, open SpotNav, the menu (three dots), *Redownload*, switch on *Show beta versions* and choose `3.0.0b8`.
3. Restart Home Assistant, then hard-refresh the browser (or reset the app's frontend cache).

Already on an earlier 3.0 beta? *Update* is enough. Going back to 2.5.x: use **2.5.5 or later**, as described in the 3.0.0b1 notes.
