SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.10.0 adds instant notifications to the SpotNav app and makes solar stop a charge the charger started by itself at once when there is no surplus.

## New

- **Instant notifications in the SpotNav app** (app 1.0.5 or later, optional and off by default). When an event you chose happens, Home Assistant wakes the phone through the SpotNav server and Google's push service, so the notification arrives at once instead of up to 15 minutes late. The wake-up is empty: nothing about your charging leaves Home Assistant. Turn it on in the app under Settings → Notifications; the diagnostics show whether a phone is registered and how the last wake-up went.

## Solar and hybrid

- **A charge the charger starts by itself is decided at once.** Many chargers, such as Easee, start charging as soon as the car is plugged in. Under Solar, or Hybrid outside a planned window, SpotNav now stops such a charge on the first reading when there is no surplus, instead of waiting several minutes as if the sun had just faded. With enough surplus it continues at the minimum current and is checked like any solar start. A Start you press, a planned window, top-off and Charge now are never stopped.

After updating, reload the browser. In the Home Assistant Companion app, force-stop the app (Android settings → Apps → Home Assistant) if the card says it cannot read the integration's answer.
