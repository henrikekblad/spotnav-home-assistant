SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.17.0 lets load balancing bring a car up to its plan's current without bouncing on the fuse, keeps the night free of new-schedule notices, gives a home battery time to make room for a solar start, and keeps every dialog's title in view.

## Load balancing

- **No bouncing on the fuse.** A step up aimed at the very edge of the fuse's headroom, and a charger that reports its own current far less often than the site meter, made a car bounce: on 17 October one stepped from 7 to 14 A, was cut to 9 and 7 A five seconds later, and the same again every minute. Now a step up stops one amp short of the edge, a level that went over is not tried again for ten minutes, a lowering waits for a charger reading taken after the last change (and while the site is over by no more than 2 A takes off only what it is over), and one site reading cuts at most once. An overload of more than 2 A is still cut at once.
- **Into a charging battery's share of a full fuse.** With **battery gives way** on, a car held at its minimum while a home battery charged from the grid and filled the fuse stayed there. Now the car steps up into the battery's share, at most 2.5 A over the limit for as long as the battery takes to answer, and the step is taken back when the battery does not make room for it.
- **The last amp to the plan.** The final step up to the plan's current, smaller than the regulator's dead band, now goes through after the usual wait, and is taken back like any other step when the battery does not give way to it. A step into the battery's share is measured from the readings before it was written, since the site meter sees the car before the charger reports it.

## Solar

- **A home battery gets time to give way.** With **Car first**, a start counted on the sun's power going into a charging home battery was stopped as soon as the car drew and the grid showed import, although many batteries take tens of seconds to lower their charge for the car. Now the battery gets a minute from when the car is seen drawing; the start is stopped as before when the surplus is still short then, and at once when the battery starts to discharge into the car.

## Notifications

- **No new-schedule notices in the night.** When load balancing changed the current a car could get, the plan's periods moved and each move was told as a new schedule. Now, once a plug-in's plan has been told (or a person has just changed the settings and seen it), a plan calculated again on the same prices during that plug-in is not told: the card and the app show it. A plan that rests on new prices, such as tomorrow's published at midday, is told as before, and so is a departure that can no longer be met, at any hour. Unplugging ends the plug-in.

## Card

- **The wait shows the plan.** The status line for a charge waiting for its planned start now also gives the plan's energy, cost and distance: *Charging waits for the planned start at 22:30 · 16.6 kWh · 17.1 kr · 87 km*.
- **Titles stay in view.** In Settings and every other dialog only the part under the header scrolls, so the title and the close button stay at the top, with a thin divider once the content has scrolled.
