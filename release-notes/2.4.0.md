SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.4.0 plans a visitor's car by kWh once you say it is a guest car.

## New

- **A guest car.** A visitor's car plugged in was taken for yours: SpotNav read your car's level and target, so it planned nothing (your car at its target) or the wrong amount. *Which car is plugged in?* now offers **Guest car** too, on the phones, in the card and in the app, and *Change car* has **Guest car** at the end of the list, also at a charger with one car. Nothing switches to a guest by itself: a person answers or chooses it. A guest's plan is an amount of energy, 10 kWh until you change it, with the charger's own strategy and departure (Cheapest within the next twelve hours when there is no departure), and nothing of a car: no level, no target, no minimum charge level, no charge limit, no plug or position sensor, no wake, and nothing learned. Once the amount is delivered the charge stops and the charger stays paused until the guest is unplugged: it does not charge on at any price, and a charge the charger begins by itself is stopped. Your *Start* still charges. Unplugged for more than two minutes, your car and its settings come back; choosing one of your cars ends the guest's plan at once, and a restart while the guest stays plugged in keeps it. The status says *Guest car · 10 kWh by 07:00* and then *Guest car · done, waiting to be unplugged*, the *New plan* notification says it is the guest car's, and the charge history names the session's car *Guest car*.
- **The guest amount.** The card's Settings has a *Guest car* tab after your cars in the Car section, for an administrator, with the one row: the amount a guest's plan starts from. Changing the amount in the Plan dialog during a guest's plan changes it too; the last change wins. In the Plan dialog a guest has the amount in place of the target, and no car level, fill, minimum level or charge limit; the departure time, the current, the strategy and *Charge now* stay. A guest leaves at the next time the departure comes round: the weekdays and a chosen date are your car's and are not shown.
- **A charger with one car asks when its car is not the one.** Identification ran only at a charger more than one car can charge at. At a charger with one car SpotNav now looks at that car's own reports too, and asks *EV6 or Guest car?* only when the car is ruled out: its plug sensor said "not plugged in" around or after the plug-in, a fresh position away from home, or it is identified at another SpotNav charger. A car that says nothing is no question.
- **The question puts Guest car where it belongs.** Android shows three buttons: with one or two cars each car and *Guest car*; with three or more, the likeliest car, *Guest car* and *Open SpotNav*. With two or more cars *Guest car* comes first when every car is ruled out; with one, the car comes first.
- **The camera can say it is none of the cars.** The AI Task is asked for one of the reference cars, a car that is none of them, or no car. *None of these cars* never makes a guest by itself: with two or more cars it puts *Guest car* first, and when the model is sure it counts as the cars with a reference picture ruled out, so a charger with one car asks. The camera is asked at a charger with one car too.
- **For the app.** The dashboard has a `guest` block, the status lines `guest_planned` and `guest_done`, and the command `update_guest`. The released app reads none of it; the webhook sends it only to an app that asks for it.
