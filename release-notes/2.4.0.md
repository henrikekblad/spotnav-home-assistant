SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.4.0 prices a grid fee that is a percentage of the spot price and a grid fee that is higher in high-load hours, and plans with both.

## New

- **A grid fee that is a percentage of the spot price.** Some network operators charge part of the grid fee as a percentage of the spot price (for example 20 öre/kWh plus 5 % of spot). The electricity price has a new addition, *Grid fee, % of spot*, off until you set it. It is added before VAT, so with 36 öre of tax, a fixed grid fee of 16 öre without VAT and 25 % VAT, a spot price of 80 öre costs (1.05 × 80 + 36 + 16) × 1.25 = 170 öre. It follows the spot price's sign: at a spot price of -10 öre the percentage is -0.5 öre, and the price is 51.9 öre. The order of the hours does not change, so plans stay where they were; the plan's cost, the price now and the charge history follow.
- **A grid fee that is higher in high-load hours.** For a time-of-use grid tariff, *Grid fee in high-load hours* is the grid fee inside a high-load window, in place of the ordinary one there. You set the window first: whole hours of the area's own clock, weekdays only or every day, November to March or all year (in Home Assistant its entities start from weekdays 07-19, November to March); the high-load fee is refused until there is one. Public holidays count as the weekdays they fall on. A plan then avoids the window where that is cheaper, the electricity price's *Cheap now* and *Expensive now* hours follow, and on the days the clocks change the window keeps to the clock on the wall.
- **In the card's Settings.** The price section shows *Grid fee, % of spot* and *Grid fee in high-load hours* under the grid fee, and the high-load hours on one line ("Weekdays 07–19, Nov–Mar") while that fee is on. Turning the high-load fee on with no hours set asks for the hours first, filled in with weekdays 07–19, November to March for you to check against your network operator's tariff, then the fee; nothing is saved until both are confirmed.
- **Set on the electricity price.** Both are part of the electricity price, so every charger that prices with it follows at once. In Home Assistant the two figures are numbers on the electricity price device; their on/off choices and the window's hours and days are there too, hidden by default. The app shows and sets them in a later release. Neither addition has a suggestion, and both are locked where the area's published price already includes the grid fee (Great Britain's Agile, Spain's PVPC). Moving the electricity price to another area switches the high-load fee off, as its figure is in the old area's unit; the figure is kept, so switching it on again is one step.

## Not covered yet

- **Winter is always November to March.** A tariff whose winter months differ, a high-load window with half hours, separate day and night fees beyond one window, and a percentage of the month's average spot price (SpotNav takes each interval's own) are not covered. A demand charge (a fee per kW of the month's peak) comes later.

## For app and card developers

- The dashboard has an additive `grid_tariff` block beside `price_source`, sent over the webhook only to an app that asks for it (`"reads": ["grid_tariff"]`), and one write: `spotnav/update_grid_tariff` over the WebSocket and the webhook action `update_grid_tariff`. The settings record is unchanged: its `overrides` rows keep their four keys, and a full settings write from a client that does not know the grid tariff leaves it as it is. `api_version` stays 1. See [Apps and API](../docs/api.md).

## Going back

- **Going back to 2.3 drops the two additions from every price, and keeps them stored.** 2.3 does not read them, so plans and prices are made without them; they are used again when you update. The two numbers and the hidden entities are left unavailable in 2.3 and can be removed there by hand.
