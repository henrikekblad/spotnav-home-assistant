SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.1.0 lets a departure name a date, holds a charge that starts by itself between planned hours, and checks many more chargers against how their integrations really behave.

## New

- **Depart on a date.** In the Plan dialog, choose "Every day" or "On a date" up to seven days ahead. For a dated departure SpotNav may wait for hours whose prices are not published yet, when the last four weeks show that those weekdays and hours are clearly cheaper, and it always keeps the departure. The card says why it waits, for example "Waiting: Sundays were 30 % cheaper the last 4 weeks".
- **Plugged in between planned hours.** Many chargers start charging as soon as a car is plugged in. With a plan in place, SpotNav now stops such a charge once and the card shows when the planned charge starts. Starting it again yourself, from the charger's app or on the charger, is respected.
- **Better diagnostics.** A charger's diagnostics now list the last 20 commands SpotNav sent, with their result and the charger's status before and after, so one file answers "why did Start do nothing".
- **A disabled charger is pointed out.** When a charger's own enable switch is off (for example Easee's, often left off by another integration), the card says so.

## Improved

- **Many chargers checked against their integrations.** OpenEVSE, GARO, ChargePoint, SMA EV Charger and SmartEVSE are now detected correctly. ABB Terra AC pauses at 0 A instead of ending the session, Webasto Next starts a new session reliably, SmartEVSE returns to your own mode, Peblar sets the current before switching on, and Ohme approves a session waiting for approval. Alfen, Heidelberg, Wattpilot and go-e report whether they are charging, and the vendor modes of Hypervolt, Charge Amps and Wallbox are shown as conflicts. Pod Point is no longer offered.
- **Site meters.** Sungrow and SolaX meters can be detected, Sungrow batteries get the right sign per connection mode, and a Perific load balancer next to a Zaptec charger is recognised: SpotNav then only starts and stops the charger and leaves its current to Perific.

## Fixes

- **A deleted charger leaves its site.** Removing a charger left it behind in the site, shown by its id, where it could hold back the other chargers. It is removed now, and one left behind by an earlier version is cleaned up after the next restart.

## Good to know

- The Android app shows the plan as before; it will show and set the departure date in its next version.
