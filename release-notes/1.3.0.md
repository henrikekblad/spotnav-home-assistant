SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.3.0 makes the settings dialogs simpler, explains measurement problems precisely, and supports chargers behind a smart plug.

## New

- **Chargers behind a smart plug.** A charger with no integration of its own can be set up with a smart plug's switch and its power sensor (W or kW). SpotNav calculates the charged energy from the power, and when the charger draws almost no power during a planned window for five minutes, the card says the car may be finished.
- **The same charger set up twice is pointed out.** Two SpotNav chargers that control one physical charger (the same device, measurement, OCPP connector or Easee device) are named in the card, in Repairs and in the setup flow. SpotNav never removes one by itself.
- **Price history for daily departures.** When tomorrow's prices are not published yet, the price history now explains a wait for them ("Waiting: Saturdays were 25 % cheaper the last 4 weeks"), and ends the wait when the hours already published are clearly cheaper than those that usually follow. Otherwise SpotNav waits for the publication exactly as before.

## Improved

- **Simpler settings dialogs.** In the site and charger dialogs you first choose the kind (one sensor or two, where the current comes from, battery, energy meter or power), and only that kind's fields are shown; the others are cleared on save. A charger's current limit and energy meter show "Automatic", "Choose an entity" or "None", and "None" now lets a charger be started and stopped without SpotNav setting its current. The technical summary at the top of the charger dialog is gone; a line under the current limit appears only when a charger restricts how its current can change.
- **Measurement problems named.** When the site's measurement cannot be used, the card says which phases lack a value or are too old, and which sensors they are.
- **A site without chargers says how to continue.** The site's setup step, its Repairs entry and the card editor explain how to add a charger to it.
- **Site notes as one list.** The site dialog lists its notes once, most important first.
