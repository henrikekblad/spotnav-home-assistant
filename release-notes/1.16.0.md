SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.16.0 lets the card show prices in kronor or öre, draws a finer chart for a card shown large, and labels the chart's time axis with plain hours.

## Card

- **Prices in the major or minor unit.** Settings → **Display** has a new **Price unit**: the market's minor unit (öre/kWh, cent/kWh), as before and the default, or its major unit (kr/kWh, €/kWh). It applies to every price per kWh the card shows: the chart's *Now*, the highest and lowest price, the price axis, the price of a point you select in the chart and the average prices in the charging history. The major unit is shown with two decimals, for example *Now 1,63 kr/kWh*. Amounts of money, such as a plan's cost, are unchanged. Like the chart's time axis, the choice belongs to the device: it is kept in the browser for every SpotNav card in it, and anyone can change it.
- **A finer chart for a card shown large.** A new card option, `chart_detail: true`, draws the price chart with finer detail for a card shown large, such as a wall screen's fullscreen view that lays the card out at its usual width and enlarges it: every hour labelled, about five or six price steps instead of three, smaller points, thinner lines and smaller axis text, and a slightly taller chart. At normal size its text is small, so leave it off on an ordinary dashboard.

  ```yaml
  type: custom:spotnav-card
  charger: 01JABCDEF0123456789ABCDEFG
  chart_detail: true
  ```

- **Plain hours on the time axis.** The chart labels its time axis with two-digit hours every three hours (00, 03, 06 … 21, 24) instead of 00:00, 06:00, 12:00, 18:00, 00:00. On a narrow card where the labels would touch they come every six hours. With **From now** the midnight in the range is still named by its day.
- **From now on a narrow card.** The last two labels of the time axis could overlap on a narrow card with **From now**; they no longer do, and the current hour's label is no longer dropped.
