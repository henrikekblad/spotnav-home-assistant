SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.0.4 shows which vehicle the charger plans for.

## New

- **The planned vehicle on the card.** Under the charger name, a quiet line shows the vehicle and its state of charge, for example "e-Outback · 92 %". When charging to a target it reads "62 % → 80 %", a charge estimated between readings is marked with "~", and a reading more than an hour old says how old it is. The line is not shown when no vehicle is chosen or the vehicle has no charge sensor.
- **Switch vehicle from the card.** Selecting the line opens a list of the charger's vehicles with their charge. Choosing one changes the vehicle the charger plans for, and users without edit rights can see the list but not change it.
