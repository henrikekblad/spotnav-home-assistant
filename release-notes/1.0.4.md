SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.0.4 shows which vehicle the charger plans for.

## New

- **The planned vehicle on the card.** Under the charger name, a quiet line shows the vehicle and its state of charge, for example "e-Outback · 92 %". When charging to a target it reads "62 % → 80 %", a charge estimated between readings is marked with "~", and a reading more than an hour old says how old it is. The line is not shown when no vehicle is chosen or the vehicle has no charge sensor.
- **Switch vehicle from the card.** Selecting the line opens a list of the charger's vehicles with their charge. Choosing one changes the vehicle the charger plans for, and users without edit rights can see the list but not change it.

## Improved

- **Settings are easier to read on a phone.** Labels no longer break in the middle, long values get their own line under the label, and labels are shown muted so the values stand out.
- **The vehicle overview shows the charge level** ("Charge level 36 %") instead of the name of the sensor it comes from; the sensor is chosen in the vehicle dialog.
- **The card updates reliably after an upgrade.** Its address now changes whenever the card itself changes, so browsers and the companion app load the new card instead of a cached one. If the app still shows an old card after this update, clear the Home Assistant app's cache once in Android's app settings.
- **Documentation:** a clearer setup order (charger first, then the site), the two ways to enter a meter manually, choosing between several vehicles, and how to collect a log when something fails. The supported hardware list now separates setups confirmed by users from those only detected from an integration's source.
