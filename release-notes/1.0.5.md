SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.0.5 tidies the site measurement dialog and writes distances in your language's unit.

## Improved

- **Distances in km or mil.** The card writes range in Scandinavian miles (mil) in Swedish and Norwegian and in kilometres in every other language, as the Android app does.
- **A clearer site measurement dialog.** It opens with one short line, the meters and batteries SpotNav found are listed only when one differs from what the site already uses (the one in use is marked "In use"), informational notes such as a voltage taken from the inverter are shown neutrally instead of as warnings, and each phase heading comes before its fields.

## Fixes

- **A detected meter the site already uses is no longer offered again.** Sites set up with an early version were missing two sign settings, which made their own meter look different from the detected one.
