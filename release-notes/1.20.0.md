SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.20.0 makes sure an overload always ends in a lower current or a stop, plans correctly across the clock changes, tries a window's start again when it did not take, and adds Mazda cars and SolaX house batteries. It also removes the OCPP pilot-floor probe.

## Load balancing

- **An overload always ends in a lower current or a stop.** A lowering the main fuse needed could be held back for up to half a minute by steps that are not about safety: yield stepping settling, the step guard, the damper, a slow meter or a membership conflict. An overload that only a pause can answer was held the same way. Now every import overload goes past those steps and ends in a lower current or a stop at once.
- **The yield ceiling waits for the battery.** With **battery gives way**, the car could step above the main fuse into a home battery's share before the battery had shown it gives way. The ceiling above the fuse now applies only once the battery has been seen to give way (a successful probe, or a step where its charge fell); until then the fuse itself is the ceiling.
- **OCPP lowerings are checked.** A charge point that rejected or ignored a lower `AssignedCurrent` still counted as lowered, so the overload stayed and the charge was never stopped. A lowering an overload needs is now read back, and a charger that does not confirm it within 5 seconds is stopped. OCPP current writes are also limited to six a minute; a lowering is never held by that limit.
- **Fewer recalculations.** The site was recalculated on every reading of every meter entity, several times a second. Readings that arrive together are now taken in one recalculation, and an overload is still acted on at once.

## Charging

- **Departures across a clock change.** The plan's last start and the hours a departure needs were reckoned in wall-clock time, so on the night the clocks change they were an hour off: in spring a need that did not fit got no plan at all and the car was not charged, in autumn a need that fitted was charged at any price, and a departure just after midnight waited for prices it did not need. They are now reckoned in real time, and no price at or after the departure is asked for.
- **A start that did not take is tried again.** A planned period's start was sent once. If it failed or the charger did not act on it (an Easee left waiting to start, a cloud error), the car stayed uncharged for the period. Now the start is tried again after 90 seconds, 5 and 10 minutes, but never when a person stopped the charge, load balancing paused it, the sun owns it, the charger's own scheduler holds it, the car refuses or the charge is finished. A start owed because the charger was not ready waits for the whole period instead of 15 minutes.
- **A start that never drew is not "the car ended the charge".** On chargers controlled through a switch, a charger that was ready but not yet charging could be read as the car refusing, and the period and the next ones were then held. Before the car has drawn, only the car's own refusal now counts.
- **The history-based wait says what it waits for.** The status now reads, for example, *Waiting for Thursday's prices (at 13:45), which have been 41 % cheaper over the last 4 weeks*, instead of a sentence that read as waiting until Thursday.

## Chargers

- **OCPP sensors found through the registry.** The connector's status, current and transaction sensors were found by their default names, which single-connector charge points and renamed sensors do not use. They are now looked up in Home Assistant's registry.
- **Easee commands reach a re-added charger.** Commands went to the charger's stored device, which no longer exists after the Easee integration was removed and added again. The device is now found at each command.
- **Peblar's held stop is told truthfully.** Beyond Peblar's three pauses in ten minutes a stop holds the charge at 6 A; SpotNav now says so, keeps the charge's owner, counts the 6 A in load balancing and tries the stop again.
- **A charger integration reload** no longer records a charge as finished and started again, or splits a session.
- **DEFA's lifetime meter** is now read by the charge-level estimate as well as found at setup.
- **The OCPP pilot-floor probe is removed.** The diagnostic that tried 5, 3 and 1 A on an OCPP charger is gone; nothing used its results.

## Cars and batteries

- **Mazda** cars through *Mazda Connected Services ng* (`mazda_cs`) are listed: the charge level, plug and location are found automatically. Confirmed with a Mazda MX-30.
- **SolaX house batteries** through *SolaX Inverter Modbus* are detected (charge-positive, SolaX devices only). Confirmed on a SolaX system. A parallel (master/slave) system still needs the battery's sensor chosen by hand.
- The supported list now marks Easee, Tibber Pulse, Mercedes-Benz and Solcast as confirmed on real hardware.
