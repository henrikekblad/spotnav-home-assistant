SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.5.5 is the safe version to go back to from the SpotNav 3.0 beta, and keeps a charge whose car the camera recognised.

**Going back from the 3.0 beta (3.0.0b1)? Install 2.5.5.** It reads what 3.0 stores for charging, so going back keeps your charge history and your settings. Going back to 2.5.4 or older after running the beta can lose the charge sessions recorded under 3.0, and going back to a version older than 2.5.3 can also reset the charger's settings. Before you go back, turn off Battery control and Control the heat: they are 3.0 features, and 2.5.5 does not run them.

## Fixes

- **A charge session whose car the camera recognised is no longer lost at a restart.** The session was stored with the camera as the way its car was decided, which the reader did not know, so the session was left out at the next start and dropped from the history, its costs and statistics at the next save. It is now read back like any other.
- **Going back from 3.0 keeps the charge sessions recorded under it.** 3.0 stores when the car was plugged in with each charge session, and 2.5.4 took such a session as unreadable: every session recorded under 3.0 disappeared from the history, costs and statistics after going back. 2.5.4 also dropped 3.0's hourly outdoor temperature and battery figures from the site's power history at its next save. 2.5.5 reads them and keeps them, so nothing is lost going back or upgrading again. It also reads past fields a later 3.0 version may add to the charger's settings, its saved charge state, camera and notifications.

**SpotNav 3.0 beta is coming soon.** The first beta of SpotNav 3.0 (house, home battery, heat pump and hot water) will be offered to those who switch on beta versions in HACS. 2.5.x stays the normal release. If you try the beta and want to go back, 2.5.5 is the safe way back.
