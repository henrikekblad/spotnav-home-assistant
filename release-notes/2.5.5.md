SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.5.5 is the safe version to go back to from the SpotNav 3.0 beta, and keeps a charge whose car the camera recognised.

**Going back from the 3.0 beta (3.0.0b1)? Install 2.5.5.** It reads everything 3.0 stores, so going back keeps your charge history and your settings. Going back to 2.5.4 or older after running the beta can lose the charge sessions recorded under 3.0. Before you go back, turn off Battery control and Control the heat: they are 3.0 features, and 2.5.5 does not run them.

## Fixes

- **A charge session whose car the camera recognised is no longer lost at a restart.** The session was stored with the camera as the way its car was decided, which the reader did not know, so the session was left out at the next start and dropped from the history, its costs and statistics at the next save. It is now read back like any other.
- **Data written by SpotNav 3.0 is read and kept.** 3.0 stores a few things 2.5.4 did not know, such as when the car was plugged in for each charge session. 2.5.4 took such a record as unreadable and dropped it, so every session recorded under 3.0 disappeared from the history after going back. 2.5.5 reads past what it does not know and writes it back unchanged, so nothing is lost when you go back, and nothing when you upgrade to 3.0 again. This covers the charge sessions, the charger's settings (3.0's departure calendar included), the charger's camera and notification settings, the charger's saved charge state, and the site's hourly power history (3.0's outdoor temperature and battery figures per hour). Everything 2.5.5 uses itself is still checked as strictly as before.

**SpotNav 3.0 beta is coming soon.** The first beta of SpotNav 3.0 (house, home battery, heat pump and hot water) will be offered to those who switch on beta versions in HACS. 2.5.x stays the normal release. If you try the beta and want to go back, 2.5.5 is the safe way back.
