SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.5.4 keeps a charge that load balancing paused from being forgotten, so it starts again as soon as the site has room.

## Fixes

- **A charge paused by load balancing at its window's start is no longer left off for the rest of the window.** A home battery began charging from the grid in the minute the plan's window opened; the car was cut, then paused. The charger kept charging for a moment, so the pause was sent a second time, and while that stop was on its way the charger reported its charge once more. SpotNav took that report as the plan's charge, which is right, but then counted it as a new start that ends the pause, which it is not: nothing is sent to the charger for it. The pause was forgotten, the charger was off with nothing waiting to resume it, and the car stood until the next window started it an hour and a half later, the last half hour and more of that with room for 19 A. Such a report now leaves the pause in place, and the charge is resumed as soon as the site has room for the charger's minimum.
- **A charge that load balancing stopped is looked at again a minute and a half later.** If the charger is then off in an open window of the plan with no pause on record and nothing else holding it (a person's Stop or Start, a reached target, the car having ended the charge, the sun owning the charger), the window's start is decided again, at most three times, as a start that did not take is. It is capped by load balancing as every start is: with no room for the charger's minimum nothing is sent, and the charge waits as a balancing pause that is resumed when there is room. Only with the car known to be plugged in, and only while the charger is off by SpotNav's own stop: a charge started or stopped at the charger since is left alone.
