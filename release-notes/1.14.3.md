SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.14.3 stops solar charging a car that never took the start, plans hybrid correctly when the sun covers the whole need, and marks tomorrow's planned charging on the price chart.

## Solar and hybrid

- **A start the car never takes ends.** When solar started a charge but the car took nothing (an Easee left at *waiting for start*, the car asleep or not accepting a charge), SpotNav went on as if it charged: it kept writing currents to the charger and the status changed between charging and *surplus fading, stopping soon* for as long as the sun shone. Now, when the car has drawn nothing five minutes after the start, solar stops and the status reads *Solar · the car stopped charging; trying again at …*, with the same retry as when the car ends a charge itself (after 30 minutes, then twice as long each time). A charger that holds the car back itself, a start still being answered and a charge a plan window owns are left alone.
- **Hybrid when the sun covers everything.** When the forecast covered the car's whole need before tomorrow's prices were published, a rounding remainder of a few billionths of a kWh was planned as energy to buy: planning failed with an error and the log filled with *Auto preview calculation failed* warnings many times a second. The status now says that the sun covers the need.
- **A repeated planning failure is logged once.** An unexpected planning failure is logged as a warning once, with its message, and repeats for the same inputs only at debug level.

## Price chart

- **Tomorrow's planned charging is hatched.** The chart shows one day with tomorrow's prices over today's, so they compare quarter by quarter. A plan that runs past midnight drew tomorrow night's part at the left edge exactly like today's, where it read as a plan already over. Tomorrow's part is now hatched, today's stays solid; the collapsed strip does the same.
- **A plan waiting for the running window.** When a new plan waits for the charging window that runs now to end, the chart showed the old schedule's later windows and hid the new plan under them. It now shows the running window, then the new plan after it, and no longer the windows the new plan replaces, as the status line says.
