SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.12.0 adds "Fill" to the energy setting, tidies the card's sliders, makes load balancing hold on to a charge it has just started, and waits for each price area's own publication time.

## New

- **Fill.** When the car's level and battery size are known, the energy slider reaches past what the battery has room for, marks "full" on the track and says how much fills it (and the car's own charge limit when it is below 100 %). Its last step is "Fill": the charge then follows the car and fills it to its limit at every plug-in, instead of a fixed amount. The status says "Charging until the car is full, 9.5 kWh now."
- **A collapsible price chart.** Click the price row above the chart to fold it into a slim timeline with the charging periods and the current time. The choice is remembered per charger in the browser; `chart: compact` in the card's YAML starts it folded.
- **Each price area's own publication time.** Great Britain (Octopus Agile, about 16:00 UK time) and Spain's regulated PVPC (about 20:15 Madrid time) are no longer expected at 13:00 Brussels time with the rest of Europe: the waiting line shows the right time, and once it has passed it names no time.
- **No "new plan" notification for your own change.** A plan that follows straight from a setting you changed (in the card, the app or an entity) is not notified; plans from new prices, plug-in or the car's level still are, and problems always are.

## Card

- The energy, target, current and charging-period sliders show their value on the label row, without a number field; a value set elsewhere (such as 7.3 kWh from the app) is shown exactly and kept unless you move the slider.
- "Finish by a deadline" reads like the other field labels.

## Charger control and load balancing

- **A charge just started is not paused for its own current.** Right after a start, the site's meter often shows the car before the charger's own reading does; load balancing now counts that rise as the car's (never more than the charger was given, for at most 90 s), so the charge is not paused for using its own current. A real overload is still lowered or paused at once.
- **A pause for the main fuse is resumed** once there is room again, while the charge is still wanted (a plan period, the sun, or your Start). Repeated pause and resume is limited, and a Stop you press is never resumed.
- **One stop per decision.** Two parts of SpotNav could send a stop a fraction of a second apart; the second met no running charge, and an OCPP charger answered "Rejected". A safety stop and your own Stop are always sent at once.
- **The area list is read again after a restart** when the relay changed it while Home Assistant was down.

## Under the hood

- A new, explicit model of who owns a charge (the plan, the sun, you, the charger itself) runs beside today's logic in shadow mode: it decides, compares and records, but controls nothing. The debug info carries what it saw; sending it with an issue helps it along. See [docs/architecture-state.md](https://github.com/henrikekblad/spotnav-home-assistant/blob/main/docs/architecture-state.md).
- New documentation: [Prices](https://github.com/henrikekblad/spotnav-home-assistant/blob/main/docs/prices.md), including the relay's published files. Documentation pictures are now in the dark theme.

After updating, reload the browser. In the Home Assistant Companion app, force-stop the app (Android settings → Apps → Home Assistant) if the card says it cannot read the integration's answer.
