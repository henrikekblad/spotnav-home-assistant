SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.12.2 stops telling you about the same plan again after Home Assistant restarts.

## Fixed

- **No "new plan" notification after a restart with the same plan.** SpotNav now remembers, per charger, the plan it last told you about (its charging periods, the energy planned and the car it is for), or showed you after a change of your own,, also across restarts and updates. A plan that comes out the same after Home Assistant starts is not notified, to your phone or to the app; any real change still is.
