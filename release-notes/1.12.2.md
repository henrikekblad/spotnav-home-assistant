SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.12.2 stops telling you about the same plan again after Home Assistant restarts.

## Fixed

- **No "new plan" notification after a restart with the same plan.** SpotNav now remembers, per charger, the last plan it told you about or that followed a change of your own (its charging periods, the energy planned and the car it is for), also across restarts and updates. A plan that comes out the same after Home Assistant starts is not notified, to your phone or to the app; any real change still is.
- **Solar follows the sun without load balancing.** On a site without active load balancing (including a site that measures current per phase, and a charger whose phase is not set) the charging current now follows the solar surplus as it should. Before, the current asked for was only recorded and the car kept charging at the current it started with. The current never goes above the charging current you set.
- **No early stop when the charger's reading lags.** A solar start that counted on the house battery's charge is no longer stopped in its first seconds just because the charger had not yet reported the car's draw.
- **Solar leaves an empty charger alone.** A charger with no car plugged in is no longer started by solar, and no longer holds on to the sun's surplus while another charger could use it. The current also no longer moves up and down by one amp on every reading when the surplus hovers at a whole amp.
