SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.14.0 gives each car a minimum charge level, splits the charge into periods by itself, sets the car's charge levels with sliders, and speaks nine languages.

## Before you update

- **Your chargers switch to automatic charge periods.** A charger that had a maximum number of charging periods set is switched to **Automatic** once, when you update. To keep a limit, choose **1 period** to **8 periods** again under the charger in the card's settings (**Charge periods**); a number chosen now stays.
- **Automations that set the number of periods need a change.** The number entity **Maximum charging periods** is removed. In its place is the select **Charge periods**, with the options `auto` and `1` to `8`. An automation or script that called `number.set_value` on the old entity must use `select.select_option` on the new one.
- **Reload the browser.** The card now carries nine languages and its file is larger. After updating, reload the browser. In the Home Assistant Companion app, force-stop the app (Android settings → Apps → Home Assistant) if the card says it cannot read the integration's answer.

## Minimum charge level

- **A floor for every car.** Each car can now have a **Minimum charge level** in the card's car settings: **Off** (the default), or 10 to 80 % in steps of 5. Like the charge target it is the car's own, the same at every charger.
- **Below it, SpotNav charges at once.** While the car's level is below its minimum, SpotNav charges straight away at the current you set, whatever the strategy: Cheapest, Solar and Hybrid do not hold it back. The status line says *Charging to the minimum level (30 %)*.
- **At the floor the strategy takes over**, with no stop and start: a planned period that is open goes on, the sun keeps the charge while its surplus does, and otherwise the charge stops. With a target, the plan covers the rest, from the minimum up to the target.
- **Your pause or Stop wins.** A pause you chose, or a Stop, holds the minimum level too. The minimum never charges past the charge target or the car's own charge limit, and it needs the car's charge level; the setting says so when the car has no level sensor. How it works is in [Target state of charge](https://github.com/henrikekblad/spotnav-home-assistant/blob/main/docs/target-soc.md#minimum-charge-level).

## Charge periods are automatic

- **No more "Max charging periods" to guess.** Every charger now plans its charge periods automatically: the cheapest quarter-hours, split only where the saving is worth another start, and each period at least half an hour. Two cheap stretches with a slightly dearer quarter-hour between them become one period; a clearly expensive peak is still skipped. How it is worked out is in [Strategies](https://github.com/henrikekblad/spotnav-home-assistant/blob/main/docs/strategies.md#cheapest).
- **Moved to the charger's settings.** **Charge periods** is now a row under the charger in the card's settings, **Automatic** or **1 period** to **8 periods**. It is no longer in the Plan dialog.

## The card

- **Sliders for the car's levels.** **Charge target**, **Minimum charge level** and **Charge limit** in the car's settings each open a slider, with the value shown large above it. The minimum level's slider starts at **Off** and stops at the charge target, the track beyond it hatched.
- **Set the car's charge limit from the card.** Where Home Assistant can write the car's own charge limit, an administrator can now change it in the card. The slider covers the range and step the car's integration takes (50 to 100 % in steps of 10 on a Kia, for example). SpotNav writes at most once a minute per car.
- **The plan names the car.** The Plan dialog is titled with the car it plans for, for example **Charging plan for EV6**, and follows the **Vehicle** choice before you save. **Charge by** is now one row, **Energy · kWh** or **Target SoC · %**; tap the value to switch.
- **One line for the car's reading.** With a target, the car's level, its charge limit and how old the reading is now share one line: *Now 75 % · Charge limit 80 % · 8 min ago*. A target that is already met, or one at the car's own limit, is said once, without a 0.0 kWh row.
- **Marks on the target slider.** Ticks mark the car's level now and its charge limit (only when the limit is below 100 %), and a car's minimum charge level shades the track up to it, with **min 30 %** under it.
- **Reference pictures as tiles.** A car's day and night reference pictures are shown as two equal tiles in its settings, cropped as they are compared. Tap either to open the reference editor.

## Solar

- **A Solar surplus sensor on every site.** Each site now has a **Solar surplus** sensor, in watts: the power a car could take from the sun at the site right now, on the same basis solar charging uses. It works whatever strategy the chargers use, also on a site with no charger, and can be used in your own dashboards and automations.
- **Plug the car in while the sun shines.** On Solar with no car plugged in, the status line no longer says it is waiting for sun. It says *Solar · surplus available (4.2 kW)* while the surplus would start a charge, and *Solar · no car plugged in* otherwise.

## Site

- **Measuring.** The site's **Capacity state** sensor calls its healthy state **Measuring**, and its new **Activity** attribute says whether active load balancing is on: *Measuring, active load balancing off*, *Measuring, active load balancing on* or *Not measuring*.
- **Not charging.** A charger that asks for no current is now shown as **Not charging** in its **Proposed current** sensor, not as below its minimum current.

## Camera identification

- **The picture of the spot now goes first.** The camera's question now sends the picture of the parking spot first and the reference pictures after it. A vision model was seen to answer "none" when a reference of an empty spot came just before the picture it was asked about.

## Languages

- **Nine languages.** The card, the setup dialogs, the entity names, the notifications and the question *Which car is plugged in?* are now in English, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish, German, Dutch, Spanish and French. Home Assistant's own dialogs and entity names were only in English and Swedish before.
- **The same words everywhere.** Each language uses one set of terms in the card, the setup, the notifications and the SpotNav app. The glossary is in [Translating SpotNav](https://github.com/henrikekblad/spotnav-home-assistant/blob/main/docs/translating.md), which also tells how to add a language.

## Fixed

- **No empty car in the plan's list.** Once a car is chosen, the Plan dialog's car list no longer offers an empty choice.
- **A quiet log with no car.** SpotNav no longer warns about a charger's missing measured current while the charger reports no car plugged in.
- **Lighter site sensors.** The site's sensors are worked out when the site changes, so Home Assistant reading them costs almost nothing.

## For SpotNav app users

SpotNav app 1.2 shows and sets the minimum charge level, the automatic charge periods and the new sliders, and words the new status lines. With an older app, charging works as before: it sees **8 periods** where Home Assistant plans automatically (saving that keeps Automatic), a charge to the minimum level as an ordinary charge, and the solar line without a car as waiting for sun.
