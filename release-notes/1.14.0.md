## Charge periods are automatic

- **No more "Max charging periods" to guess.** Every charger now plans its charge periods automatically: the cheapest quarter-hours, split only where the saving is worth another start, and each period at least half an hour. Two cheap stretches with a slightly dearer quarter-hour between them become one period; a clearly expensive peak is still skipped. How it is worked out is in [Strategies](https://github.com/henrikekblad/spotnav-home-assistant/blob/main/docs/strategies.md#cheapest).
- **Your earlier number is replaced once.** A charger that had a maximum number of periods set is switched to **Automatic** when you update. To keep a limit, choose **1 period** to **8 periods** again under the charger in the card's settings (**Charge periods**); a number chosen now stays.
- **Moved to the charger's settings.** The setting is no longer in the Plan dialog. In Home Assistant it is the select **Charge periods**, which replaces the number entity **Maximum charging periods**.
