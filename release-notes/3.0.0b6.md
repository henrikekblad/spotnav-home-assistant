SpotNav for Home Assistant 3.0.0b6 is the sixth beta of 3.0. **Learns your home. Plans your energy.**

This is still a beta, offered only to those who switch on beta versions in HACS (or the Marketplace). 2.5.x stays the normal release. Everything new that could write to a battery, an inverter or a car still starts by only watching, or behind its own opt-in, as in the earlier betas.

## What is new

- **Battery control for more brands, tested together with you.** SpotNav now has control recipes for FoxESS, EcoFlow PowerOcean, Marstek, Solis (through RC force, with the inverter's own timeout as a guard), Kostal, Fronius and Deye/Sunsynk (limits only), beside the earlier SolarEdge, Huawei, SolaX, Solinteg, Victron, GoodWe, Tesla Powerwall and Ferroamp. For every brand but Sigenergy, control is switched on in three steps: **Become a tester** in the battery section (your e-mail address, used only for this), a **hardware test** you start once (it runs by itself between 08:00 and 21:00 with a phone notice that has a Stop button, and always hands the battery back), and a **control code** sent to you after the test's log has been looked at. Until then SpotNav reads the battery and plans in shadow, and the card says why control is not on yet. A plant whose control already ran keeps it. After a firmware, integration or SpotNav update the battery is checked again by itself; control never stops silently. See [Becoming a tester](docs/home-battery.md#becoming-a-tester).
- **No export at negative prices, for more inverters.** A new switch, **No export at negative prices**, in the Solar section sets the site's export limit to 0 kW in the quarter hours where selling would cost money, and puts it back afterwards. It works with or without a home battery, for Victron, Sungrow, Solis, GoodWe and SolaX beside Sigenergy, and is shown only where SpotNav finds the export limit. While it is off, the row shows what exporting at negative prices cost over the last 30 days. In this beta it writes only on sites with **Beta tester** on; a Sigenergy site whose battery control already set the limit gets the switch turned on and keeps it. A plant run by its own optimiser is only watched. See [No export at negative prices](docs/solar-export.md).
- **A Tesla that will not start charging.** When the car refuses the offered current, SpotNav turns on the car's charge switch once per plug-in (Tessie, Teslemetry or Tesla Fleet), never off, and tells the phones if the car still takes nothing.
- **Battery losses counted where they happen.** Batteries that report their power on the DC side (Sigenergy among them) are now counted on the AC side in the review, the Now tab, the flow and the plan, with the sun's charge over a hybrid's DC bus as its own round trip. The inverter's standby is learned, and the inverter has its own slice in the house pie. This changes earlier comparisons: a battery's own optimiser can no longer look better than the best possible plan.
- **The Info tab** has one baseline (no battery) in the day table, columns named by who controlled the battery, a row a month in the year view, and says so on days SpotNav did not control the battery.
- **Mirrored batteries.** A home battery mirrored from another Home Assistant through Remote Home-Assistant is read, read only.
- **The SpotNav app gets the card's words from Home Assistant**, so a wording fix reaches the app without an app update.

## Fixes

- **Battery hand-back:** each unread setting now waits on its own before a hand-back writes, and the wait starts over at every hand-back; a stored hand-back whose brand is no longer known is kept and handed back when it returns, never lost; a test asked for during a running hardware test is refused.
- **A grid charge right after a sale** with the car held no longer starts at the sale's rate.
- **Load balancing:** after a charger could not take a lower current in time, a margin below the fuse is kept for a week, scaled with the charger's write interval.
- **Estimated heat** is never more than the house measured (the flow's House node could vanish).
- **The solar forecast's review** leaves out impossible production hours and days the panels produced nothing under a sunny forecast (snow).
- **A failed write with no error text** now names the error's type.

## What to do after updating

- **Battery owners (not Sigenergy):** open the battery section. If you want SpotNav to control the battery, press **Become a tester**; nothing is written before the hardware test has passed and the control code is entered.
- **Solar owners:** look at **No export at negative prices** under Solar. Switch it on if you are a beta tester and want it.
- **After the update, reload the card:** hard-refresh the browser (Ctrl+Shift+R). In Home Assistant's companion app, reset its frontend cache (Android: Settings → Companion app → Troubleshooting → Reset frontend cache; iPhone and iPad: Settings → Companion app → Debugging → Reset frontend cache), then close the app fully and open it again. See [Troubleshooting](docs/troubleshooting.md).

## Known limits

- Of the battery recipes, only Sigenergy has run on a real plant. Every other brand waits for its first tester.
- The export limit has run only on Sigenergy; the five new inverters are untested and write only on beta sites.
- The SpotNav app shows the battery's tester stage but has no sign-up or code entry: those are done in Home Assistant by an administrator.

## Before you downgrade

Going back to 3.0.0b5: the export switch, a tester sign-up and a control code are not read there; battery control works there as it did in 3.0.0b5. Coming back to 3.0.0b6 later, they are read again.

## How to get the beta

1. **Make a Home Assistant backup first** (Settings → System → Backups).
2. In HACS, open SpotNav, the menu (three dots), *Redownload*, switch on *Show beta versions* and choose `3.0.0b6`.
3. Restart Home Assistant, then hard-refresh the browser (or reset the app's frontend cache).

Already on an earlier 3.0 beta? *Update* is enough. Going back to 2.5.x: use **2.5.5 or later**, as described in the 3.0.0b1 notes.
