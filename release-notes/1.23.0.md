SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.23.0 brings phase switching for solar to more chargers, a card that fills a box of fixed height, and fixes after a restart.

## Solar charging

- **Phase switching for more chargers.** The setting **Phase switching for solar** (the card's settings, under the charger) is now offered for more chargers than Peblar, on a charger wired for three phases on a site with solar, as before:
  - **Lektrico**, three-phase model: its *Force single phase* switch.
  - **NRGkick**: its phase count, set to 1 or 3. Not with a single-phase connector.
  - **go-e Charger**, through either the API v2 or the MQTT integration: its phase switch mode, set to one or three phases (never left on Auto).
  - **Fronius Wattpilot**: its *Phase Switch* select, set to 1 Phase or 3 Phases (never left on Auto).
  - **Easee**, at the start only: Easee takes a phase change only at a pause, and the change reaches every charger on its circuit. So an Easee charger is offered the setting only when it is the one Easee charger on its circuit, and its phases are set only right before a solar start, never while it charges. A charge that runs follows the rules without switching, and starts at Easee's start minimum of 7 A as before.
  - None of these has been tried on a real charger yet. If you try one, please tell us how it went in an issue.
- **A switch the charger reports.** Lektrico, go-e and Wattpilot say when they are switching phases; while they do, the paused charge is not taken for the car ending it, as in the 90 seconds after a switch.
- **A failed switch is no error.** A phase switch whose call fails is now given up for the plug-in like one the charger does not confirm, and the charge goes on.

## Card

- **A card of fixed height uses all of it.** In a box of fixed height (a wall-screen layout, a sections view card given a number of rows), the chart takes the spare height, the buttons stay at the bottom, and a long status is cut with "…" (the whole text shows on hover) once the chart is at its smallest. Elsewhere the card looks as before.

## Fixes

- **Notices across a restart.** A restart of Home Assistant no longer loses the notice about a new plan that was about to be sent, sends a *plan at risk* notice again that you already had, or names a stop in an open window wrongly.
- **Phases after a charger reboot.** A charger that forgets its single-phase setting at a reboot is reckoned on three phases again at the next look; while a phase switch is settling, SpotNav waits for it before it believes what the charger reports.
- **Load balancing by one name.** The site's *Activity* sensor now says *Measuring, load balancing on* and *off*, as the card and the site's settings do. An automation that compares the sensor's state is not affected: the state values are the same.
- **A rollback raises no Repairs issue.** Going back to this release from a later one reads the charge in progress without a failure.

## Under the hood

- The new charge logic, run alongside the old for a few releases, now keeps the safety hold, the stops after your Stop and load balancing's memory itself. Nothing should look different; if a charge starts or stops at a time you did not expect, a debug bundle helps us.
