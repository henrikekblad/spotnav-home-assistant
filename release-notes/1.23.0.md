## Solar charging

- **Phase switching for more chargers.** The setting **Phase switching for solar** (the card's settings, under the charger) is now offered for more chargers than Peblar, on a charger wired for three phases on a site with solar, as before:
  - **Lektrico**, three-phase model: its *Force single phase* switch.
  - **NRGkick**: its phase count, set to 1 or 3. Not with a single-phase connector.
  - **go-e Charger**, through either the API v2 or the MQTT integration: its phase switch mode, set to one or three phases (never left on Auto).
  - **Fronius Wattpilot**: its *Phase Switch* select, set to 1 Phase or 3 Phases (never left on Auto).
  - **Easee**, at the start only: Easee takes a phase change only at a pause, and the change reaches every charger on its circuit. So an Easee charger is offered the setting only when it is the one Easee charger on its circuit, and its phases are set only right before a solar start, never while it charges. A charge that runs follows the rules without switching, and starts at Easee's start minimum of 7 A as before.
  - None of these has been tried on a real charger yet. If you try one, please tell us how it went in an issue.
- **A switch the charger reports.** Lektrico, go-e and Wattpilot say when they are switching phases; while they do, the paused charge is not taken for the car ending it, as in the 90 seconds after a switch.
- **A failed switch is no error.** A phase switch whose call fails is now given up for the plug-in like one the charger does not confirm, and the charge goes on.
