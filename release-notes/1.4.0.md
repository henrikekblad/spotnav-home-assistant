SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.4.0 recognises more meters and chargers, warns about other integrations that control the same charger, supports 230 V IT networks, and fixes several problems new users ran into.

## New

- **More meters and inverters found by themselves.** Home Assistant's new SolarEdge Modbus integration (beside the HACS one of the same name), the Bitvis Power Hub HAN reader (with its total grid power, so solar and hybrid work), the Solis Modbus meter, Cozify HAN, the Ferroamp grid current and the frient electricity meter over ZHA.
- **Other integrations that control chargers are pointed out.** When EV Smart Charging, EVSE Load Balancer or PeaqEV also controls a charger, the charger setup and the card say so: turn it off for that charger, or the two will fight.
- **230 V between phases (IT network).** A site, or a charger without one, has a "Voltage between phases" setting: 400 V or 230 V. Three-phase power, and with it the plan and the solar maths, follows it. In Norway 230 V is suggested.
- **More vehicles recognised**, among them Smartcar, Lucid, Cupra, Volkswagen ID, Lynk & Co, Zeekr, smart, ABRP and TeslaFi.

## Fixes

- **A charge already running is no longer stopped after a Home Assistant restart.** The charger's control reads unavailable while it reconnects; that is no longer taken for a new plug-in.
- **A renamed charge control is followed**, and a removed or disabled one is reported as unavailable instead of looking healthy.
- **Commands to a charger give up after 30 seconds** (45 s for cloud integrations), so one hanging call cannot block every start, stop and current change.
- **The price area is suggested later** if the price server could not be reached during setup.
- **A charger whose phases cannot be read asks for them** (1 or 3) instead of assuming 3.
- **An Easee charger can be set up while evcc or openWB is installed.** The setup now warns about them but still suggests what it found.
- **A safety margin at or above the main fuse is refused.**
- Pairing requests are limited, and a malformed request to the app's webhook gets a clear error.
