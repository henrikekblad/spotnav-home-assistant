SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.7.0 closes a load-balancing gap on OCPP chargers, lets a home battery that charges from the grid share the main fuse with the car, and tidies the card's header and start-up.

## Safety

- **An OCPP charger is paused when the fuse leaves less than its minimum current.** Before, load balancing sent nothing to an OCPP charger below 6 A, so the charge kept its current. It is now paused through its charge control, like other chargers, and resumed once there is room again, but only while the charge is still wanted (inside a plan window, or while solar wants it; never after a Stop, a Pause, a window's end or solar turning off).
- **A charge starts at no more than the fuse allows.** With active control on, a plan's start is capped at the site's headroom, and does not start at all below the charger's minimum.

## New

- **A home battery charging from the grid no longer starves the car.** With "car first" and yield stepping on, SpotNav starts the car at its minimum to see whether the battery gives way, and stops it again within 30 seconds if the grid does not come back under the fuse (then waits 10 minutes, doubling to an hour). While the battery charges and holds the grid at the fuse limit, the car is no longer stepped down for readings a fraction of an amp over it. Real overloads are handled exactly as before; "house battery first" is unchanged.
- **"Starting up…" after a restart.** Until the charger and the solar forecast have reported (at most three minutes), the card says it is starting up and keeps its buttons still, instead of showing a wrong state.
- **The card's header** has History and Settings only; "About this card" moved into Settings. On a phone the name, the car's line and the buttons fit on one row again; in a very narrow card the buttons move together to their own row.

## Fixes

- One invalid price area from the price server no longer hides every area.
- The warning about a charger's missing measured current is no longer logged at every start-up.

After updating, reload the browser (or clear the app's frontend cache) so the new card loads.
