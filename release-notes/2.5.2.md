SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.5.2 keeps a car that was cut at the main fuse running until the meter has reported the cut.

## Fixes

- **A cut at the main fuse is no longer followed by a needless stop when the charger's own reading arrives before the meter's.** A house load put a phase 6 A over the limit and the car was cut from 18 A to 9 A, as it should be. Four seconds later the charger reported its new 8.8 A while the meter had not reported since: reckoned against that, the same site reading made the house 17.6 A, left 2.4 A for the car, and the car was stopped, to be started again a minute later. The site reading a cut was made on now makes that one cut, a pause included, as long as the charger's own reading shows the cut took; the next reading of the phase decides as before, and a charger that still reads above what was written, or a new overload on another phase, is cut again at once.
- **With two cars, SpotNav no longer picks the wrong car when one car's cloud answers slowly with an old position.** The Kia's cloud answered the plug-in's re-read a few seconds late with where the car had been parked hours before; when SpotNav asked again three minutes later, that old answer was taken as the car reporting itself away, the other car was chosen, and the Kia's real answer a second later (home, plugged in) was ignored. SpotNav now waits for the cars to answer before it decides, never takes an old answer as a new report, and switches to the right car when it reports itself plugged in.
- **A home battery that limits the grid by power no longer makes SpotNav back the car off on days with low mains voltage.** A battery that holds the grid at the main fuse by its power (20 A at 230 V) reads a little more current when the voltage is low: at 225 V the same power is about 20.5 A. That was just outside what SpotNav accepted as the battery's own regulation, so the car was stepped down to a pause and its restart failed. Where the meter measures each phase's voltage, SpotNav now allows for what that power draws at the voltage measured. The bound: at most 0.5 A plus 5 % of the limit (the main fuse less the safety margin) above that limit, and only while a home battery charges from the grid and the phase's voltage is measured below 230 V; anything above that is still lowered at once, as before.
- **In a dashboard box of fixed height the card shows more of a long status sentence.** The chart now gives way down to 100 px before the sentence is cut short, and the change-car button no longer moves onto a row of its own when the car's line is just too wide.
- **The card no longer says the car is finished while SpotNav is waiting to start the plan.** Some chargers report their session finished once SpotNav stops the charge to wait for a cheaper hour (OCPP "Finishing"); the card said "Finished" until 21:15 although the car was at 45 % of a 95 % target. The card now says "Waiting" then, and "Finished" only when the car is proven done: it stopped taking current or ended the charge itself, it reached its target, or the plan's need is met. A charger that says finished without such proof, while SpotNav holds nothing, reads "Connected". The charger's status now stands after its name in the header ("HALO Charger · Waiting"), or as a small symbol right after the name when the card is too narrow for the word. How the car was identified is a small icon after the car's name (camera, cable, position, chosen manually or assumed, with the words when you point at it), turning while the car is being identified, and a narrow card no longer starts a row of the car's line with a lone "·".
