SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.5.2 keeps a car that was cut at the main fuse running until the meter has reported the cut.

## Fixes

- **A cut at the main fuse is no longer followed by a needless stop when the charger's own reading arrives before the meter's.** A house load put a phase 6 A over the limit and the car was cut from 18 A to 9 A, as it should be. Four seconds later the charger reported its new 8.8 A while the meter had not reported since: reckoned against that, the same site reading made the house 17.6 A, left 2.4 A for the car, and the car was stopped, to be started again a minute later. The site reading a cut was made on now makes that one cut, a pause included, as long as the charger's own reading shows the cut took; the next reading of the phase decides as before, and a charger that still reads above what was written is cut again at once.
- **With two cars, SpotNav no longer picks the wrong car when one car's cloud answers slowly with an old position.** The Kia's cloud answered the plug-in's re-read a few seconds late with where the car had been parked hours before; when SpotNav asked again three minutes later, that old answer was taken as the car reporting itself away, the other car was chosen, and the Kia's real answer a second later (home, plugged in) was ignored. SpotNav now waits for the cars to answer before it decides, never takes an old answer as a new report, and switches to the right car when it reports itself plugged in.
- **A home battery that limits the grid by power no longer makes SpotNav back the car off on days with low mains voltage.** A battery that holds the grid at the main fuse by its power (20 A at 230 V) reads a little more current when the voltage is low: at 225 V the same power is about 20.5 A. That was just outside what SpotNav accepted as the battery's own regulation, so the car was stepped down to a pause and its restart failed. Where the meter measures each phase's voltage, SpotNav now allows for what that power draws at the voltage measured (at most 5 % of the fuse); anything above that is still lowered at once, as before.
