SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.6.0 shows the charger's status in the card, stops a charge that runs outside the plan, prices imported history by itself, and fixes the import/export sensor setup.

## New

- **The charger's status in the card.** The line under the charger's name ends with its status: Not connected, Connected, Charging, Paused, Finished or Error. It is read from the charger itself (OCPP connector status, Easee, Zaptec, Wallbox, go-e and others); a value SpotNav does not recognise shows nothing.
- **Imported history gets its prices by itself.** Earlier charges imported without a price are priced again every night while Home Assistant runs, so they fill in as older price days become available, without a restart.
- **A warning when the grid power sign looks wrong.** When the site's meter is one SpotNav recognises (for example SolaX) and "The sensor shows export as positive" is set the other way, the Site dialog says so.
- **Clearer setup summaries.** The Site found and Charger found steps list what was found with bold labels and one line per phase.

## Fixes

- **A charge left running outside the plan is stopped.** When a new plan replaced a running window, or Home Assistant restarted outside every window, a charge SpotNav had started could keep running. SpotNav now remembers that it started the charge, also across a restart, and stops it outside the plan. A charge someone started by hand is still left alone.
- **"Import and export as two sensors" is never saved half-filled.** Every export sensor must be chosen; before, the dialog could save one sensor and turn "export as positive" off without a word. Under the choice it now says that a meter without export per phase should keep one sensor with direction.
- **A full car is no longer reported as a problem.** When the car is full and the need is met, the card shows only the normal status line.
- **The card loads faster and more reliably.** The card file is smaller, and a card with Home Assistant's own options (size in the sections view, visibility) no longer shows "Configuration error".
- **Narrow cards** keep the charger's and the car's names whole; the buttons move to a row of their own.
- The yield-stepping ceiling in the site options is shown rounded.

After updating, reload the browser (or clear the app's frontend cache) so the new card loads.
